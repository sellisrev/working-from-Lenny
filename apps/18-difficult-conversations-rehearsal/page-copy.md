## Block 1: Value first

The working interface. On `/difficult-conversations`:

A short setup, then the rehearsal runs in chat.

1. **Which conversation?** Poor performance / a peer who keeps going around you / a layoff / cutting a committed feature on a customer / killing an exec's pet project / opening a PIP.
2. **Your history with this person** (optional). Calibrates how the counterparty reacts.
3. **Mode.** Rehearse (you practice, the coach plays the other person) / show-me (just give me the version that holds up) / fournier (paste your intended opening and contrast it with canonical leadership guidance).

Submit button: "Start the rehearsal."

The rehearsal (in chat):
- The coach opens in character, reacting the way that person realistically would.
- You respond. It pushes back once, fairly.
- After at most three of your turns, it breaks character and gives you:
  - **How that landed**: empathy, clarity, decisiveness, letting silence work, and leaving the person's dignity intact.
  - **Where you weakened**: the single most important fix.
  - **The version that holds up** for this scenario, with the hard sentence and the silence beat marked.
  - In fournier mode: what to keep, what to cut, and the one move you're missing.

## Block 2: Description, concrete → general

### 2a. What this app does for you

The hardest part of a hard conversation usually isn't the sentence. It's the four seconds after it, when most people start talking again to ease their own discomfort and take the moment away from the other person. Pick the conversation you're dreading, and rehearse it with a coach who plays the counterparty, pushes back fairly, and then scores you on the things that actually matter: did you lead with empathy, was the message unmistakable, did you own the decision instead of hiding behind "the company," did you let silence work, and did the other person leave with their dignity intact. You walk away with the version that holds up, with the silence beat marked. Built for managers and leads facing a layoff, a PIP, a cut feature, or a hard piece of feedback.

### 2b. How it works

Like the saying-no rehearsal it's built on, this holds a real conversation without any special inference machinery: the chat assistant runs the role-play directly from a rehearsal brief the tool hands it (the scenario's realistic reaction, a five-part rubric, the canonical handling with the silence beat, and a turn limit). Nothing tracks the back-and-forth behind the scenes; the conversation itself is the state. The scenarios and the canonical handlings are grounded in `difficult-conversations.md`, `communicating-bad-news.md`, `giving-feedback-as-leader.md`, and `performance-reviews.md`. The "what would Camille Fournier do" mode contrasts your intended opening against canonical leadership guidance, naming her only where the corpus actually quotes her. The tool remembers your setup, not the transcript; text only; inputs stay local.
