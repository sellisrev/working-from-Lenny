## 3–4 word hint

| Hint | Read |
|---|---|
| **Five objections, named** | Promises the deliverable shape directly. The "named" word does the work — visitors recognize that a generic critique would not be named, only a specific one. **Recommended.** |
| Before the board sees | Situational, hints at urgency without spelling out the format. |
| Rumelt would not approve | Inside-baseball; only lands for visitors who recognize the reference. |
| What would kill this | Cribbed from the spec's "what would kill this in six months" objection; works as a hint but loses the volume signal. |
