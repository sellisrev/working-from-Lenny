**Door 1.** Most universal of the Phase 0 set: works for any PM regardless of company stage or seniority, and has the viral "I scored X/20" shareability shape. Best fit for the first tile a visitor lands on.

## 3–4 word hint

| Hint | Read |
|---|---|
| **Hopefully fewer than ten** | Playful, hints at "score" without giving away the format. Pairs with the viral "I scored 14/20" shape — visitors recognize the framing after they click. **Recommended.** |
| Twenty mirrors, briefly | Slightly cryptic but works; hints at self-reflection. |
| How many do you? | Open-ended hook; intriguing but might read as unfinished. |
| The honest self-audit | Most literal; loses the playfulness. |
