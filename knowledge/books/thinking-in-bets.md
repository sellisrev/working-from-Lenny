# Thinking in Bets — Annie Duke

## Consensus key insights

Most influential decision-making framework in the corpus. Duke's poker-derived framework for decisions under uncertainty.

1. **Resulting** — the cognitive bias of judging decision quality by outcome quality. A good decision can have a bad outcome (bad luck) and a bad decision can have a good outcome (good luck). Most people, including most managers, judge decisions by outcomes alone.

2. **Calibrate uncertainty.** Treat decisions as bets: assign probability ranges, force yourself to commit to estimates, track them. Over time, calibration improves.

3. **Pre-mortem.** Before committing to a decision, imagine it has failed in 18 months. What killed it? The exercise reveals load-bearing assumptions and buried risks.

4. **Kill criteria up front.** Define in advance what would cause you to stop. Without explicit kill criteria, sunk cost takes over and bad decisions get extended.

5. **Mortal vs. immortal feedback loops.** Some decisions reveal feedback quickly (this hand of poker), others slowly (this hire over 2 years). Adapt your decision process to the feedback rate.

6. **Seek truth, not affirmation.** Ego protection prevents calibration. Seek decision groups (small, trusted) where being shown wrong is the goal, not the cost.

## Potentially interesting but rarer takes

**[CRITICAL READING REQUIRED]**

- **"Treating every decision as a bet."** Some readers absolutize this. For low-stakes Type-2 (reversible) decisions, the bet framing is overhead; just decide. Cf. Bezos's reversibility test.
- **"Probability estimates beat intuition."** The framework's strongest in domains with large samples (poker, hiring, bets). For one-off strategic decisions, intuition + framework together beats either alone.

## Where the consensus may have decayed

- **The framework is durable.** The corpus and external authoritative voices haven't substantially updated Duke's framing.
- **For AI products**, Duke's framework applies cleanly: decision-vs-outcome separation, kill criteria for AI bets, pre-mortems on AI features. No decay observed.
