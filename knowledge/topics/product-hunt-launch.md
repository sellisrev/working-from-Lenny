# Launching on Product Hunt

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "How to successfully launch on Product Hunt (when it's right for your startup)" (2024) + Ryan Hoover's Product Hunt episodes (2022).

### When Product Hunt makes sense
- **Consumer or prosumer products** that benefit from tech-audience visibility.
- **Products with a clear hook.** PH audience is attention-driven; the visible value-prop has to land fast.
- **You have community.** Coordinated launch-day support from your existing community amplifies the launch.
- **You're early-stage and need awareness.** PH is most valuable for products without distribution; less valuable post-PMF.

### When Product Hunt doesn't make sense
- **Enterprise / deep-vertical B2B.** PH audience overlap is small.
- **Products requiring high-touch onboarding.** PH visitors sample products briefly; complex setup loses them.
- **Products with no community.** Without coordinated support, the launch lands quietly.

### Tactical patterns (Lenny + Ryan Hoover)
- **Launch on a Tuesday-Thursday.** Mondays are launch-saturated; Fridays / weekends quiet.
- **Pre-launch hunt (someone hunts you).** Builds anticipation.
- **Coordinate early supporters.** First few hours' votes matter disproportionately.
- **Engage in comments.** PH community values founder presence.
- **Day-of media + social blitz.** PH alone doesn't sustain attention; layered launches work better.

### Common failure modes
- **Treating PH as the launch.** PH is one channel; not the launch.
- **Vote-buying / inauthentic boosting.** PH detects and penalizes.
- **No follow-up.** PH spike → quiet → no real growth. Must convert visitors into users / community.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **PH has lost some relevance** as direct social channels (X, LinkedIn, Substack) became primary launch channels.
- **AI-product launches** are common but saturated; PH attention is harder to capture.
- **Founder-as-launcher** (cf. [pr-and-press](pr-and-press.md), [content-driven-growth](content-driven-growth.md)) often outperforms PH-as-only-launch.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a founder considering PH launch**: not always the right channel. Think about target audience.
- **If you're a marketer**: PH is one event; not a strategy.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022**: Ryan Hoover (PH founder) on Lenny's Podcast.
- **2024**: Lenny "How to successfully launch on Product Hunt" — clear-eyed about when it works.
- **2025-2026**: PH relevance fades; direct channels rise.
