# The art of the pivot

## Current consensus

Lenny's two-part series "The art of the pivot" (2024) is the corpus-canonical reference. Reinforced by multiple founder pivot stories (Tom Conrad, Varun Mohan / Windsurf, Eric Simons / Bolt).

### Successful pivot pattern (Lenny part 1: definitive list)
- **Pivots usually retain something.** Team, technology, customer base, market expertise. Pure pivots from nothing-shared rarely succeed.
- **Pivots happen on observed signal.** Not panic; not exhaustion. Specific signals from the market.
- **The new direction often was visible in the old data.** Pivot decisions look obvious in retrospect because the seed was there.
- **Speed matters.** Once the decision is made, execute fast. Slow pivots burn cash and morale.

### When and why to pivot (Lenny part 2)
- **Customer signal saying you're not solving a real problem.** Multiple users churning; renewals falling; word-of-mouth not happening.
- **Team signal saying the work isn't getting better.** Velocity dropping; conviction eroding; multiple senior team departures.
- **Market signal that the inflection has passed or shifted.** What worked at PMF doesn't scale; or the inflection you bet on isn't materializing.
- **Better adjacent opportunity that uses your same assets.** Tom Conrad / multiple-pivot stories.

### How to pivot well
1. **Be honest with yourself first.** Founders often resist pivot signals for months.
2. **Be honest with team second.** Pivot decisions made in isolation produce departures.
3. **Communicate to investors third.** Some will support the pivot; some won't. Pivot is often when boards turn over.
4. **Be honest with customers fourth.** Existing customers deserve a transition plan.
5. **Cut the old work fast.** Half-pivots fail.
6. **Don't pivot too often.** Pivot fatigue burns out the team.

### Examples in the corpus
- **Eric Simons / Bolt**: near-death pivot to AI-coding; $40M ARR in 5 months post-pivot.
- **Varun Mohan / Codeium → Windsurf**: pivot from infrastructure to IDE-native AI.
- **Tom Conrad**: serial pivot stories (Pandora, Quibi, Pets.com, Snap).

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Pivots are faster.** AI tooling lets teams test new directions in weeks not quarters.
- **AI-themed pivots are common.** Many 2024-2025 startups pivoted to add AI as a primary value-add. Cf. cautions on pivot-as-thesis-laundering — adding "AI" to a tired idea isn't a pivot; it's a relabel.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a founder considering pivoting**: read both parts of Lenny's series + the Eric Simons / Varun Mohan founder stories.
- **If you're an investor whose portfolio is pivoting**: support speed-of-pivot, not delay. The cost of delay usually exceeds the cost of imperfect pivot direction.
- **If you're an existing employee at a pivoting company**: ask for the rationale; ask for the runway; decide whether to stay with calibration. Pivots survive when the team commits; struggle when they don't.
