# GTM motions across B2B SaaS

## Current consensus

The corpus consensus, distilled from Lenny's "GTM motions of 30 B2B SaaS companies" (2022, foundational), Jeanne DeWitt Grosser (Vercel, Stripe, Google) on "world-class GTM in 2026", and adjacent episodes:

### The four canonical GTM motions
1. **Sales-led / enterprise** — outbound + inbound demand; AE-driven close. Slack-Enterprise, Workday, Snowflake's enterprise tier. High ACV.
2. **Product-led / self-serve** — user signs up, activates, converts without sales touch. Notion, Figma, Slack's free tier. Low CAC, requires viral or organic growth.
3. **Bottom-up + sales overlay** — PLG to start, then add sales for expansion. Stripe, Datadog, Twilio. The dominant 2020s pattern for high-growth B2B.
4. **Product-led sales (PLS)** — sales is triggered by product signal (heavy usage, expansion intent, etc.) rather than outbound. Pete Kazanjy, Hila Qu canonical references.

### What determines the right motion
- **ACV size**: < $5k strongly favors PLG; > $50k strongly favors sales-led.
- **Time-to-value**: short (< 1 hour) favors PLG; long (weeks to set up) favors sales-led.
- **Buyer count**: single-decision-maker favors PLG; committee favors sales.
- **Compliance / security gating**: gated favors sales-led even at low ACV.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Hybrid is the default.** Pure PLG and pure sales-led are increasingly rare for venture-backed B2B at scale; hybrid bottom-up + sales overlay dominates.
- **AI-native GTM**: Lovable, Cursor, Bolt have demonstrated bottom-up consumer-prosumer GTM in B2B-adjacent tools. Lower CAC than traditional B2B; faster scale.
- **Founder-led sales until $300-500k ARR** is durable advice (Lenny B2B series).

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a sales / GTM leader at a startup**: pick the motion that fits your buyer reality, not your preference. PLG hires fail in sales-led contexts; sales hires fail in PLG contexts.
- **If you're an investor evaluating B2B**: ask "what's the dominant motion and why?" If the answer is "we do everything," the GTM strategy is unfocused.
- **If you're a CFO**: GTM motion determines unit economics. A switch from sales-led to PLG can cut CAC 5-10x; a switch the other way can multiply it. Each has its place.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021-2022**: Lenny — GTM motions of 30 B2B SaaS companies; foundational.
- **2022-2023**: Pete Kazanjy on founder-led sales; Hila Qu on PLG / PLS.
- **2024-2026**: Jeanne DeWitt Grosser ("world-class GTM in 2026"); Brian Tolkin (Opendoor); Asha Sharma on enterprise AI GTM.
