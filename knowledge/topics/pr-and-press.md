# Getting press and PR for your product

## Current consensus

Anchored in Emilie Gerber ("The ultimate guide to PR", 2023) + Jason Feifer ("How to get press for your product", 2023) + Lulu Cheng Meservey (Substack, "Navigating comms and PR", 2023).

### What works
1. **Newsworthiness over newsworthy framing.** Real news (real launch, real number, real human story) beats spin.
2. **Build journalist relationships pre-need.** When you have news, you want a journalist who already knows you.
3. **Specific journalist for specific story.** Pitching wide produces nothing; pitching to the journalist who covers your beat works.
4. **Founder voice (Lulu Cheng Meservey framing).** Direct social channels (X, Substack, podcasts) often outperform earned media for B2B.
5. **Patience.** Most pitches don't land. Volume and quality both matter; don't get discouraged.

### Lulu Cheng Meservey's "go direct" framing (Substack)
- **Founders should own their narrative.** Don't outsource voice to a PR agency.
- **Direct social + community + email** are increasingly the primary channels; earned media is a layer on top.
- **Go-direct doesn't mean skip-PR.** Both work together.

### Emilie Gerber's PR canon
- **Pre-launch prep takes weeks.** Most PR efforts fail because the prep was rushed.
- **Embargoes and exclusives** — useful when used sparingly; toxic if misused.
- **Press is one channel; not THE channel.** Many great companies launch with no press; some get massive press but few users.

### Jason Feifer (Entrepreneur magazine) framing
- **Editors want stories, not announcements.** "We launched X" is not a story; "X solves problem Y for audience Z" might be.
- **Local angle, big stakes.** Stories that have specific human stakes get covered.
- **Make it easy to say yes.** Pre-written quotes, images, data — reduce the journalist's work.

### Common failure modes
- **PR-as-substitute-for-product.** Press doesn't fix bad product.
- **Saying yes to bad coverage.** Bad press can hurt.
- **Spammy mass pitches.** "Spray and pray" produces nothing and burns goodwill.
- **No follow-up.** Journalists are busy; thoughtful follow-up converts pitches that initially didn't land.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Earned media's reach has shrunk.** Direct channels (X, LinkedIn, Substack, podcasts) reach larger audiences than most magazines.
- **Founder-as-media** is increasingly common. Marc Benioff, Tobi Lutke, Brian Chesky all run direct channels.
- **AEO matters for press.** Articles that mention you contribute to AI-engine citation; quality articles in low-traffic outlets can still produce AEO benefit.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO**: invest in your direct voice (X, LinkedIn, podcast guesting) as primary; earned media as supplement.
- **If you're a CMO**: PR strategy should align with brand and category-creation work; not isolated.
- **If you're a sales / marketing lead at a startup**: most early-stage startups don't need a PR firm. They need a founder who's good at direct communication.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2023**: Emilie Gerber + Jason Feifer + Lulu Cheng Meservey on Lenny's Podcast — corpus PR canon.
- **2024-2026**: Direct channels overtake earned media for many B2B contexts; AEO emerges as new PR consideration.
