# Writing great job postings

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "Examples of great job postings" (2021) — the corpus-canonical post for this topic.

### What makes a job posting great
1. **Specific role context.** Not "we're hiring a PM"; "we're hiring a PM for our growth team focused on activation and onboarding."
2. **Honest about what's hard.** Top candidates value honesty; sanitized listings under-attract.
3. **Inverts the funnel.** Top candidates will read carefully; the listing's job is to disqualify wrong-fit candidates as much as attract right-fit ones.
4. **Concrete about what success looks like.** Not "drive growth"; "increase activation rate from 18% to 25% in 12 months."
5. **Distinctive voice.** Generic listings produce generic candidates.

### Examples in the corpus (Lenny 2021)
- Stripe's classic engineering listings — specific, opinionated, brand-aligned.
- Notion's brand-driven listings — make joining feel like joining a movement.
- Figma's design role listings — specific about the craft bar.

### Common failure modes
- **Buzzword-driven copy.** "Self-starter, fast-paced environment, wear many hats" repels good candidates.
- **Long requirement lists.** 30-line requirement lists discourage qualified candidates from applying.
- **No salary range.** Increasingly required by law in many jurisdictions; also a candidate-experience signal.
- **Compensation hidden behind multiple interviews.** Discloses too late; wastes everyone's time.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-fluency requirements** are increasingly explicit. "Comfortable with Cursor / Claude Code" is a new standard line.
- **Salary transparency mandates** are spreading; embrace them.
- **Description-of-the-team-culture matters more.** Cf. cautions on Matt MacInnis ([`../cautions/leadership-style.md`](../cautions/leadership-style.md)) — companies known for soul-crushing culture are losing talent.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a hiring manager**: invest in your job postings. They're a brand artifact; bad ones lose top candidates.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: salary transparency is now table stakes; embrace it as candidate-experience competitive advantage.
- **If you're a candidate**: a well-written job posting is signal of company quality. A buzzword-stuffed one is a red flag.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021-08**: Lenny "Examples of great job postings" — corpus foundational.
- **2024-2026**: AI-fluency requirements + salary transparency + culture-credibility expectations rise.
