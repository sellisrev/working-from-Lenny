# AI state of the union and inflection points

## Current consensus

Anchored in Simon Willison ("An AI state of the union: We've passed the inflection point", 2026) + Marc Andreessen ("The real AI boom hasn't even started yet", 2026) + various lab-insider episodes (Karina Nguyen / OpenAI, Mike Krieger / Anthropic, Kevin Weil / OpenAI).

### Where we are (May 2026 corpus snapshot)
- **Past the toy stage.** AI products have crossed from experiment to production. Cursor at $300M ARR, Lovable at $10M in 60 days, Mercor at $500M in 17 months — these are not anomalies.
- **Capability inflection** (Simon Willison): Sonnet 4.6 / Opus 4.6 (Nov-Dec 2025) was the moment many developers shifted from "interesting" to "indispensable."
- **Distribution moats reshaping.** Cf. [distribution-as-moat](distribution-as-moat.md). AI commoditizes software; distribution becomes the durable moat.
- **Org-design implications.** Cf. [ai-org-restructuring](ai-org-restructuring.md). Companies restructuring around AI-native cycle times.

### What corpus voices think comes next
- **Simon Willison**: "dark factories are coming" — automated workflows that don't require humans in the loop.
- **Marc Andreessen**: "the real AI boom hasn't even started yet" — current state is opening salvo.
- **Asha Sharma (Microsoft)**: "agents will outnumber employees by 2026" — agentic workforce framing.
- **Nikhyl Singhal**: 30k shed / 8k AI-first hire in 12-24 months at major tech companies.

### The contested questions
- **AGI timelines**: corpus voices range from "soon" to "decades." Anthropic / OpenAI insiders generally optimistic; academic and skeptical voices more cautious.
- **Productivity gains durable vs. transient**: Intercom 2x throughput is real; whether it scales further or plateaus is open.
- **Job displacement aggregate**: corpus consensus is bifurcation (builders win, information-movers lose), not wholesale displacement.

### Inflection points the corpus has marked
- **ChatGPT public release (Nov 2022)**: started everything.
- **Cursor reaches $300M ARR (April 2025)**: AI-tooling proves SaaS-scale viability.
- **Sonnet 4.6 / Opus 4.6 (Nov-Dec 2025)**: capability inflection.
- **Code with Claude event (May 2026)**: agent primitives (routines, Outcomes, Dreams) become platform-level.

### Cautions
- **Many guests have conflicts of interest.** Anthropic / OpenAI guests are selling AI; their predictions are advocacy. Cf. [`../cautions/100-percent-claude-code.md`](../cautions/100-percent-claude-code.md).
- **The "by end of year" promise pattern** has recurred for 3+ years. Some predictions land; many don't. Calibrate.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO planning 18-month strategy**: assume the technology continues to improve at recent pace. Plan for that, not for a plateau.
- **If you're an investor**: AI-application-layer is more crowded than infrastructure. Crowded markets have higher mortality.
- **If you're an individual making career decisions**: builder track is the durable bet. Cf. [ai-pm-skills](ai-pm-skills.md), [becoming-senior-pm](becoming-senior-pm.md).

## How thinking has evolved

- **2024**: Karina Nguyen, Logan Kilpatrick, Marily Nika episodes establish the optimistic-insider perspective.
- **2025**: Reality checks (Schulhoff, Verna 4.0) plus continued capability inflections.
- **2026**: Multiple "state of the union" framings from across the corpus; Simon Willison's "passed the inflection point" framing canonical.
