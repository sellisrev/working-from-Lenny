# Activation metrics and onboarding conversion

## Current consensus

Activation is the moment a new user does the thing that proves the product is working for them. The corpus consensus:

1. **Activation = "aha moment, quantified."** Lenny / corpus consensus: pick the action that best correlates with long-term retention. For Slack, "messages sent in teams of 3+." For Twitter, "follows N accounts." For SaaS, often a configuration milestone.
2. **The aha moment isn't the activation moment**. Cat Wu's framing for AI products (cf. [vibe-coding](vibe-coding.md)) updates this: there can be a "wow moment" (first impression) and a "aha moment" (problem solved) separately. Both matter; neither alone is enough.
3. **Activation rate benchmarks vary wildly by category.** "What is a good activation rate" (Lenny, 2022): typical SaaS 20-40%, consumer subscription 5-25%, B2B with sales-led 40-60%+. Compare against your category, not a universal number.
4. **Onboarding owns activation.** Lauryn Isford ([Mastering onboarding](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/mastering-onboarding-lauryn-isford), 2023): the first session is where most retention is lost. Activation is the metric; onboarding is where you move it.
5. **Friction can help, not hurt.** The Equals freemium case study + onboarding research: some friction filters for committed users and improves long-term retention. Cf. [retention](retention.md), [freemium-vs-trial](freemium-vs-trial.md).

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-product activation has new shapes.** Vibe-coding tools generate a "wow moment" in 30 seconds (first prototype renders), then a separate "stickiness" cliff at the third or fourth session when capability limits show. Two distinct activation funnels per user.
- **Subsidized activation distorts.** Free-tier AI products show artificially high activation; the durable rate emerges only when payment is required.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a designer**: activation is a UX problem first. The aha moment must be visible in the first 1-3 sessions; if it isn't, redesign the path.
- **If you're a CFO or board member**: activation rate is the leading indicator for retention is the leading indicator for revenue. Track upstream.
- **If you're a marketing lead**: don't optimize TOFU acquisition without checking that newly-acquired users activate. Acquiring users into a leaky activation step wastes budget.
