# Continuous discovery and customer interviews

## Current consensus

Teresa Torres's *Continuous Discovery Habits* is the canonical reference. The corpus practice: weekly customer interviews are the input to good product decisions; teams that don't do them ship the wrong things more often.

### The discipline
1. **Weekly interviews.** Aim for 5+ user conversations per week per product team.
2. **Story-based interviewing.** Ask about past behavior ("tell me about the last time you needed to do X"), not hypothetical future ("would you use this if it had Y?").
3. **Cross-functional participation.** PM, designer, engineer all in the room (or async listening). Reduces handoff friction.
4. **Synthesize weekly.** Findings become inputs to roadmap; not just notes in a doc.

### Why story-based interviewing works
- **Hypothetical answers are unreliable.** People say what they think you want to hear.
- **Past behavior reveals constraints.** "Last time I needed X, I did Y" surfaces both the need and the workaround.
- **Specifics produce credibility.** Stories with names, dates, costs are more accurate than abstract opinions.

### The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) layer
- **Don't ask "would you use this?"** Ask about pain in current solution.
- **Watch for compliments.** "I'd love that" is usually politeness, not commitment.
- **Push for specifics.** Numbers, deadlines, money spent.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented interview prep + analysis.** AI helps draft questions, summarize transcripts, cluster themes. Cuts overhead.
- **Live demo + interview combo.** Cf. [ai-prototyping](ai-prototyping.md). Ship a vibe-coded prototype to the interview; get reactions to working software, not slides.
- **Eval-driven discovery for AI features.** Cf. [evals-for-ai-products](evals-for-ai-products.md). Discovery + eval + ship cycle compresses to days.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an engineer**: ask to attend customer interviews. The patterns you'll spot in their workflows will inform your engineering decisions for years.
- **If you're a designer**: continuous discovery is core design craft. Push to participate.
- **If you're a CEO at a startup not doing this**: it's the highest-leverage process change you can make.
- **If you're a sales / customer-success lead**: your existing customer conversations are continuous discovery raw material; partner with PM to extract value.
