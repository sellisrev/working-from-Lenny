# State of the PM job market

## Current consensus (April 2026 snapshot)

Lenny's tracked PM job market data has shown a counterintuitive trajectory:
- **2023-mid 2024**: tight market; layoffs; PM hiring depressed.
- **Late 2024**: AI PM roles rose sharply; aggregate hiring still recovering.
- **2025**: gradual recovery; AI PM roles dominant.
- **April 2026**: **most open PM roles globally in 3+ years.** ([State of the product job market in 2025](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/state-of-the-product-job-market-in-2025) and updates).

### What's true
- **AI PM roles are the largest growth category.** Lenny's data: 456 open AI PM roles tracked, 178 at AI-first companies.
- **Prompt engineer roles still exist but are folding into AI engineer / context engineer**.
- **Builder PMs (cf. Singhal)** are aggressively hired; information mover PMs are not.
- **The bifurcation Singhal describes is real in the data**: companies are shedding while hiring.

### What's contested
- **Wholesale "PM is dead" rhetoric** (Keith Rabois, etc.) is contradicted by the open-roles data. Cf. [`../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md`](../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md).
- **Comp**: top-end up; mid-tier flat-to-down. Bay Area regaining favor.
- **Layoffs ahead**: Singhal predicts 30k shed / 8k AI-first hire dynamic over 12-24 months.

### How to interpret as a candidate
- **If you're a builder PM** (ships, prototypes, owns outcomes): job market is favorable.
- **If you're an information-mover PM**: harder; consider repositioning your narrative around shipped impact, building AI fluency, or transitioning to a builder role.
- **If you're entering PM from an adjacent function**: the AI-fluency table-stakes makes engineering / design backgrounds more valuable than ever.

### How to interpret as a hiring manager
- Strong builder PMs are scarce; expect competitive offers and counter-offers.
- The "we'll find someone" assumption fails for top-quartile candidates.
- Investment in candidate experience (fast process, transparent timelines, custom offer decks per Brandon Hill) returns disproportionately.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-companies dominate top-of-market PM hiring.** Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, Lovable, Vercel, Mercor, Cognition all hiring aggressively.
- **Founder track is more legitimate**: 14 of 125 in Singhal's head-of-product community founded companies in the last year.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CFO budgeting headcount**: PM headcount in 2026 should reflect builder-PM economics, not orchestrator-PM economics. Fewer, better-paid PMs is the durable bet.
- **If you're an investor evaluating a hiring plan**: hiring 5+ PMs at <$5M ARR is usually a red flag; hiring 1-2 builder PMs at $1-2M ARR is reasonable.
- **If you're an exec watching the market for talent**: the strongest PMs are increasingly leaving for founder roles. Retention matters more than recruitment.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2024-09**: Lenny "State of the product job market, part 2" — first major data piece.
- **2025**: Continued tracking; AI PM roles emerge as dominant category.
- **2026-04**: Most open PM roles in 3+ years; bifurcation framing crystallized (Singhal).
