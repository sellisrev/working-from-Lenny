# Bootstrapping a business

## Current consensus

Anchored in Patrick Campbell ("10 lessons on bootstrapping a $200m business", 2023), Jason Fried (37signals contrarian), Andrew Wilkinson (75+ businesses), and Brian Halligan (HubSpot).

### The case for bootstrapping
1. **No dilution.** Founder retains ownership.
2. **Forced focus on real economics.** Without venture pressure to chase hypergrowth, businesses optimize for actual profitability.
3. **Slower but compounding.** Many bootstrapped businesses hit $50M+ revenue with no outside capital.
4. **No exit pressure.** You can hold; pass to family; sell when it suits you.
5. **Better customer alignment.** Without venture pressure to land-and-expand, you can serve customers' actual needs.

### The case against bootstrapping
1. **Slower scale.** Capital-intensive markets can't be bootstrapped easily.
2. **Founder cash flow risk.** Living on sub-market salary while building.
3. **Network effects work against you.** Network-based markets often go to whoever has the most capital.
4. **Less learning velocity.** Venture-backed companies often hire and learn faster.

### Patrick Campbell's lessons (10 from $200M ProfitWell)
- **Profitability from year 1.** Built into the operating model.
- **Pricing as a core competency.** ProfitWell built its business around pricing expertise.
- **Customer retention > acquisition.** Bootstrapped economics depend on retention.
- **Hire slowly.** Each hire is a personal cash decision, not a venture decision.
- **Patience.** Bootstrapping is years of compounding; not breakthrough events.

### Jason Fried (37signals) framing
- **Calm company.** Sustainable pace, not crunch culture.
- **Customer-funded.** Profit pays for growth.
- **Independent.** Not optimizing for sale or IPO.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI tooling makes bootstrapping more viable.** A solo founder + AI tools can build what required a team in 2020. Cf. [vibe-coding](vibe-coding.md), [ai-coding-agents](ai-coding-agents.md). Anton Osika's Lovable went $0 → $10M ARR in 60 days with 15 people.
- **The bootstrapped + AI-native combo is producing notable winners** (Lovable, Cursor, others).

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're considering bootstrapping**: read Patrick Campbell's playbook + Jason Fried's writing. Calibrate your market context.
- **If you're an existing PM considering quitting to start something**: bootstrapping is a more realistic option in 2026 than 2018 thanks to AI tooling.
- **If you're an investor**: bootstrapped companies often re-enter the venture market at Series B/C with disproportionate strength. Worth tracking even if uninvestable at seed.
