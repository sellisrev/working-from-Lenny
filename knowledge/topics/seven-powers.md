# 7 Powers and business strategy

## Current consensus

Hamilton Helmer's *7 Powers* is one of the two corpus-canonical strategy frameworks (paired with Rumelt's [Good Strategy / Bad Strategy](good-strategy-rumelt.md)). For the book digest see [`../books/seven-powers.md`](../books/seven-powers.md). This topic file cross-references the framework when invoked elsewhere.

### The 7 Powers
1. **Scale economies**: cost advantage from being big.
2. **Network effects**: each user makes the product more valuable to others.
3. **Counter-positioning**: incumbents can't copy you because doing so damages their existing business.
4. **Switching costs**: customers can't easily move.
5. **Branding**: durable price premium from reputation.
6. **Cornered resource**: exclusive access to something competitors lack.
7. **Process power**: ways of working competitors can't replicate.

### Power = Benefit × Barrier
- **Benefit**: the advantage your product delivers vs. alternatives.
- **Barrier**: the reason competitors can't replicate.
- **Both required.** A benefit without a barrier erodes; a barrier with no benefit is worthless.

### When to invoke this framework
- **Strategy review**: which of the 7 are you building?
- **Competitive analysis**: which power does the competitor have?
- **Investor pitching**: name the power(s) explicitly.
- **Pivot evaluation**: does the new direction give you access to a different power?

### Distribution-as-moat (Spiegel 2026) and the 7 Powers
Cf. [distribution-as-moat](distribution-as-moat.md). Distribution maps to several powers depending on context: network effects, branding, cornered resource, scale economies. Helmer's framework absorbs the 2026 distribution emphasis.

### Common failure modes
- **"We have all 7."** No company does. If you can't pick 1-2, you don't actually have a power.
- **"Better product" as power.** Better is not durable; the power has to be the structural barrier.
- **Power-without-benefit.** Long-term competitor moats with weak benefit deliver poor returns.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Foundation-model access is not a durable power** for most application-layer products.
- **Data-as-moat (Shaun Clowes, cf. [ai-data-as-moat](ai-data-as-moat.md))** maps to combinations of cornered resource + scale economies.
- **AI-era distribution** maps to network effects + branding.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO articulating strategy**: name the power explicitly. Cf. [assessing-product-strategy-as-board](assessing-product-strategy-as-board.md).
- **If you're an investor**: 7-Powers analysis is a fast strategy diagnostic. Use it.
- **If you're an exec evaluating a competitor**: their power is either real or imagined; figure out which.

## How thinking has evolved

- **Pre-corpus**: Helmer publishes *7 Powers* (2016).
- **2023-09**: Hamilton Helmer on Lenny's Podcast — corpus-canonical episode.
- **2025-2026**: AI-era moat reshape; 7 Powers framework absorbs but interpretation evolves.
