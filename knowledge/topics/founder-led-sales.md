# Founder-led sales

## Current consensus

Pete Kazanjy ("Founding Sales", *Founding Sales* book) is corpus-canonical. Reinforced by Lenny B2B series + Jen Abel (2025) + multiple founder episodes.

### Why founders should sell early
1. **Customer discovery is sales.** Real conversations with real prospects expose what to build.
2. **No playbook to delegate.** The founder builds the playbook through repetition; AEs run the playbook.
3. **Founder credibility closes deals.** Early customers buy the founder vision plus the product.
4. **Pricing and packaging emerge from sales conversations.** Cf. Madhavan Ramanujam — willingness to pay surfaces in selling.
5. **Cheaper than hiring an AE who fails.** First sales hires often fail without a working playbook; founder-led sales avoids the cost.

### When to transition out
- **$300-500k ARR** (Lenny B2B series) is the corpus rule of thumb.
- **When founder is the bottleneck.** If founder time becomes the limit on growth, hire an AE.
- **When the playbook is documented.** Once a senior AE can reproduce the founder's pitch, hire.

### Pete Kazanjy's "Founding Sales" canon
- **Outbound until customers come inbound.** Cold outbound is the early-stage sales motion.
- **Specific customer profiles.** Sell to a defined ICP; expand later.
- **Document everything.** Each call's objections, language, decision factors.
- **Discount aggressively early; raise prices later.** Get the first 10 customers; tighten pricing once playbook works.

### Common failure modes
- **Hiring AEs before $300k ARR.** Predictable failure; founder still has to coach the AE through every deal.
- **Using founder credibility for unscalable promises.** Custom features, custom pricing — the founder might deliver but the org won't.
- **Treating sales as beneath the founder.** Cf. Marc Benioff and others: most successful B2B founders sold for years.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented prospect research** is now standard. Founders can prepare for sales calls 5x faster.
- **Vibe-coded demos** (cf. [ai-prototyping](ai-prototyping.md)) substitute for slide decks in many sales conversations.
- **Founder-led sales now includes shipping prototypes mid-call.** Live customization in some cases.
- **Jen Abel "Sell the alpha, not the feature"** (2025) for the post-founder-led $1-10M ARR phase.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO pre-PMF**: stop hiring; start selling. The founder-led-sales-period is non-delegable.
- **If you're a sales hire joining a startup**: ask how the founder is selling. If they aren't, you're being asked to invent the playbook on your own — high risk.
- **If you're an investor**: ask what the founder's average win rate is. A founder who hasn't closed real deals is missing essential signal.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022-12**: Pete Kazanjy on Lenny's Podcast — corpus-canonical founder-led-sales episode.
- **2023-10**: Lenny B2B series part 6 — founder-led sales until $300-500k ARR.
- **2024-2026**: AI-augmented sales tooling; Jen Abel "Sell the alpha not the feature" post-founder phase.
