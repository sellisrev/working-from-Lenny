# Opportunity solution tree (Teresa Torres)

## Current consensus

Teresa Torres's opportunity solution tree (OST) is the corpus-canonical model for continuous discovery. Structure:

- **Outcome** (top): the business outcome the team is trying to achieve (e.g., "increase trial-to-paid conversion").
- **Opportunities** (middle layer): user needs / pain points / desires that, if addressed, would move the outcome.
- **Solutions** (next layer): specific solution ideas for each opportunity.
- **Experiments** (bottom): tests that validate or invalidate solutions.

### Why the OST works
- **Connects business outcome to user need.** Solutions never exist independent of the user need they serve; experiments never exist without a hypothesis.
- **Forces multiple options.** Each opportunity should have 2-3 candidate solutions; each solution should have at least one experiment plan. Prevents "solution fixation."
- **Visible structure.** The whole team can see the tree; debate happens at the right node, not at the wrong abstraction level.

### Continuous discovery (Torres, *Continuous Discovery Habits*)
- **Weekly customer interviews.** 5+ per week, ideally per PM-designer-engineer trio.
- **Story-based interviewing.** Past behavior, not hypothetical future behavior.
- **Cross-functional discovery.** PM, designer, engineer all participate. Reduces handoff friction.

### Common failure modes
- **OST as a one-time exercise.** The tree is a living artifact; updated weekly as evidence comes in.
- **Treating opportunities as solutions.** "Better onboarding" is a solution; "users abandon onboarding at step 3" is the opportunity. Conflating these defeats the framework.
- **Skipping experiments.** Solutions without experiment plans are wishes, not work.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI prototyping reduces experiment cost.** OST experiments that took weeks now take hours. The framework is unchanged; the cycle time compresses.
- **Eval-driven discovery for AI products.** Cf. [evals-for-ai-products](evals-for-ai-products.md). For AI features, the experiment is often "build the feature; measure with eval suite." OST adapts cleanly.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a designer**: continuous discovery puts you in the customer-conversation loop, which strengthens your design work. Push to participate.
- **If you're an engineer**: weekly interviews aren't a PM task; they're a team discipline. Ask to be part of the rotation.
- **If you're a CEO at a startup not doing continuous discovery**: probably the highest-leverage process change you can make. The cost (a few hours/week per team) is tiny; the upside (better decisions, fewer false starts) is large.

## How thinking has evolved

- **Pre-corpus**: Teresa Torres's blog and book (*Continuous Discovery Habits*) predate Lenny's coverage.
- **2022**: Teresa Torres on Lenny's Podcast — corpus-canonical OST + interviewing methodology.
- **2024-2025**: AI-prototyping compresses OST experiment timelines.
