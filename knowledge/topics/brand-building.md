# Brand building

## Current consensus

Anchored in Arielle Jackson ("The art of building legendary brands", 2022) + Laura Modi ("How to build a cult-like brand", 2023) + Barbra Gago (category creation + brand building).

### What brand actually is
- **Brand = perception of who you are in the customer's head.** Not a logo; not a tagline.
- **Built through consistency over years.** Brand investment compounds; one-off campaigns rarely move the needle.
- **Hard to fake.** Customers detect performative authenticity quickly.

### Arielle Jackson's framing
- **Brand is built at every customer touchpoint.** Product, support, content, community — all contribute.
- **Brand requires opinionated leadership.** Brands that try to please everyone end up bland.
- **Pre-Series A focus matters.** Founders who invest in brand at seed have advantages later.

### Laura Modi / Bobbie ("cult-like brand")
- **Customers feel ownership of cult brands.** They evangelize unprompted.
- **Cult brands have strong values that some customers reject.** Universal-appeal brands are weak; opinionated brands are strong.
- **Storytelling at scale.** Founder voice + community voice + customer voice combined.

### Barbra Gago framing (category creation + brand)
- **Brand and category creation reinforce each other.** When you create a category, your brand is associated with it.
- **Brand investment IS category education.** Most "brand spend" is actually market education spend.

### Common failure modes
- **Brand-as-decoration.** Logo redesign without product / message change is expensive cosmetic work.
- **Inconsistency.** Different voice on Twitter vs in support vs in product → confused brand.
- **Pleasing everyone.** Cult brands say no to non-fits; weaker brands chase any audience.
- **Underinvestment in early stages.** Brands built late are catching up forever.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-era brand mentions in answer engines** (cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md)) increase the value of distinctive brands. Generic brands get summarized away; distinctive brands get cited.
- **Founder-as-brand** continues to dominate AI-era startups. Founder voice on social drives brand recognition.
- **Distribution-as-moat** (cf. [distribution-as-moat](distribution-as-moat.md)) is brand's structural cousin.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CMO or brand lead**: brand work is multi-year. Don't expect quarterly ROI.
- **If you're a CEO**: founder voice is brand. Investing time in personal voice / writing / podcasting compounds.
- **If you're a CFO**: brand spend is hard to attribute but durable. Don't cut brand budget first.

## How thinking has evolved
