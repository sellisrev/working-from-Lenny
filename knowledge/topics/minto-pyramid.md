# Minto Pyramid Principle and SCR Framework

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "The Minto Pyramid Principle and the SCR Framework" (2021) + reinforced in Lenny "Essential reading for product builders—part 2" (2025) where Barbara Minto's "Executive Communication" via Michael Dearing makes the canonical reading list.

### The Minto Pyramid
A structure for executive communication:
1. **Top of pyramid**: the answer / recommendation. State first.
2. **Supporting points**: 3-4 reasons / evidence groups.
3. **Detail**: under each supporting point, the data, examples, sub-arguments.

### The SCR (Situation-Complication-Resolution) format
A specific application of the Pyramid:
- **Situation**: the state of affairs. Fact-based, unambiguous, agreed upon.
- **Complication**: what changed; what's making things harder. The pivot.
- **Resolution**: what we should do (the answer).

### Why Minto's framework works
- **Mirrors how executives process information.** They want the answer first; details second.
- **Forces clarity.** You can't pyramid-structure something you don't understand.
- **Enables disagreement at the right altitude.** Reviewers can challenge the top recommendation OR specific supporting points OR specific detail; the structure makes the disagreement productive.

### When NOT to lead with the answer
- **Sales presentations.** Sometimes you build to the conclusion.
- **Storytelling contexts.** Cf. [storytelling](storytelling.md). Story arcs reveal the answer at the right moment.
- **Negotiations.** Anchoring effects; sometimes don't reveal the answer first.

### Common failure modes
- **Burying the lede.** First sentence isn't the answer.
- **Pyramid without supporting structure.** Top point asserted; supporting points generic.
- **Too many supporting points.** 3-4 is the limit; 10 dilutes.
- **Pretending you have a recommendation when you don't.** If you're genuinely uncertain, say so; don't fake conviction.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Async-first communication** (memos, docs) rewards Minto-style structure even more than meetings did.
- **AI tools can draft pyramid-structured docs** in seconds; revise but recognize the structure works.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an exec receiving communications**: Minto-structured docs are easier to act on; ask reports to use the structure.
- **If you're a manager training reports on writing**: this is the most-leverage framework to teach.
- **If you're a PM writing PRDs / decision docs**: cf. [reviewing-prds-and-1-pagers](reviewing-prds-and-1-pagers.md), [prds-and-1-pagers](prds-and-1-pagers.md). Pyramid structure works.
