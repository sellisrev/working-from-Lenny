# Living a long and healthy life as a builder

## Current consensus

Lenny "A builder's guide to living a long and healthy life" (2025) is the corpus's recent synthesis of longevity-aware practices for tech professionals. Reinforced by Hilary Gridley, Jonny Miller, and Chip Conley episodes.

### The basics (boring, durable, dominant)
1. **Sleep is the first lever.** Rabois's "8 hours" framing is right even if his "no days off" framing isn't (cf. [`../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md`](../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md)). Most longevity research converges on 7-9 hours.
2. **Movement matters more than which kind.** Walking, lifting, sport, anything done consistently beats anything done occasionally.
3. **Resistance training preserves muscle past 40.** Building muscle is harder later; preserving it is essential.
4. **Diet: eat real food, mostly plants, not too much.** Michael Pollan synthesis is durable; specific diet wars (keto vs vegan vs Mediterranean) matter less than the basics.
5. **Stress regulation.** Cf. [burnout-and-resilience](burnout-and-resilience.md). Chronic stress shortens lifespan.

### Specific tools / practices the corpus mentions
- **Eight Sleep** (referenced by Keith Rabois as an investor) — sleep tech.
- **Continuous glucose monitoring** for personalized diet response.
- **Annual full-body MRI / blood panels** for early detection (Prenuvo, Function Health).
- **Strength training programming** (corpus references Hilary Gridley / Whoop content).

### The corpus's notable warnings
- **Don't substitute biohacking for the basics.** Sleep + exercise + diet outperform most supplement / wearable plays.
- **Don't normalize burnout as "high performance."** Cf. cautions on Keith Rabois "no days off" and Matt MacInnis "soul crushing... by design" framings.
- **Survivorship bias is real.** Successful people in tech often had advantages besides their stated practices.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Wearables and metrics have improved.** Whoop, Apple Watch, Oura — better signal density.
- **AI-augmented health coaching** is emerging; quality varies.
- **Longevity is mainstream.** Not just biohacker fringe; major tech companies offer programs.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager**: your team's health is partly a structural problem. Schedules that allow sleep + exercise + recovery aren't "nice to have."
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: longevity-related benefits (medical screening, mental health, recovery time) are increasingly important retention levers.
- **If you're an individual at any age**: the basics dominate. Don't optimize edges before the basics are solid.
