# Enterprise sales playbook ($1M to $10M ARR)

## Current consensus

The transition from "founder-led $0-$500k ARR" to "first sales team $500k-$10M ARR" is one of the most failure-prone transitions for B2B startups. Corpus consensus:

### The canonical sequence (Lenny B2B series + Pete Kazanjy + Jen Abel)
1. **Founder-led until $300-500k ARR.** Customer count usually 5-15.
2. **Hire one hungry senior AE.** Not a VP; not a junior; a senior AE who acts like a hungry founder. They'll dial, gather insights, close deals themselves.
3. **Build a working playbook with the AE.** Document what wins; what objections appear; what the buying process looks like.
4. **At ~$1M ARR, hire AE #2 to validate the playbook is repeatable.** Two AEs running the playbook successfully is the signal to scale.
5. **At $2-5M ARR, hire VP Sales** if the model is working; this is when you systematize.
6. **Killer sales pitch construction**: cf. Andy Raskin's strategic narrative work; problem framing → stakes → vision → evidence → CTA.

### Jen Abel's "Sell the alpha, not the feature" (2026)
- For $1M-$10M ARR enterprise: lead with the value differentiation (the alpha) that competitors can't match.
- Features are commodity; the durable difference is "what only we deliver."
- Sales narrative should anchor in customer outcomes, not capability lists.

### Common failure modes
- **Hiring VP Sales too early.** A VP without a working playbook can't make one; they need one to scale.
- **Hiring 5+ AEs simultaneously.** If the playbook isn't working with 1-2, more reps won't fix it; they'll dilute attention.
- **Letting sales drift from product.** PM and sales must stay tightly coupled; cf. [working-with-pms-as-sales](working-with-pms-as-sales.md).

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI agents in sales execution.** Jason Lemkin "We replaced our sales team with 20 AI agents" episode (2026-01) — controversial. Worth reading + skeptical attention to top comments (cf. [audience pushback notes](../cautions/)). The honest read: AI augments senior AEs; AI replacements at scale work for low-ACV transactional sales but unproven for $50k+ ACV consultative deals.
- **AI-augmented prospect research** is table stakes. AEs not using AI for account research are at a disadvantage.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a founder ready to hire your first AE**: senior, hungry, fewer features, more closing experience. Resist the "VP Sales" pitch from candidates with bigger titles than you can productively use.
- **If you're a VP Sales joining a startup**: ask for the working playbook. If the answer is "we want you to build it," the company isn't ready for you yet.
- **If you're an investor reviewing the sales hiring plan**: if the company is hiring 5+ AEs at <$2M ARR, that's a red flag. Sequencing matters.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021-2022**: Pete Kazanjy founder-led sales canon.
- **2023-10**: Lenny B2B series part 6 (hiring early team), part 7 (scaling growth).
- **2024-2025**: Sahil Mansuri, Matt Dixon (*The Challenger Sale*) episodes deepen the playbook.
- **2025-2026**: Jen Abel "Sell the alpha, not the feature"; Jason Lemkin "20 AI agents" episode.
