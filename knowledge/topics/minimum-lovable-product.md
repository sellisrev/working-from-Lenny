# Minimum lovable products

## Current consensus

Anchored in Jiaona Zhang ("Building minimum lovable products", Webflow, 2023) — a corrective reframing of MVP (minimum viable product) from the Cagan / SVPG canon.

### MVP vs MLP
- **MVP**: minimum viable product. Smallest thing that proves the thesis. Often ugly, broken, low-quality.
- **MLP**: minimum lovable product. Smallest thing users actually love. Higher quality bar; same minimal scope.

### Why MLP matters
- **MVPs miss the love test.** Users tolerate MVP for novelty; long-term, they need to love.
- **MLP forces craft early.** Designing for love at minimum scope produces better long-term product instincts.
- **In crowded markets, MLP beats MVP.** When competitors are good, "viable" isn't enough; "lovable" is the bar.

### What MLP requires
- **Sharp scope.** Cut features ruthlessly; quality the remaining ones to high standard.
- **Design investment.** Cf. [product-design-craft](product-design-craft.md). Beautiful within constraints.
- **Onboarding investment.** First-impression quality matters as much as feature quality.

### Common failure modes
- **MLP-as-excuse-for-perfectionism.** Don't use "we want it to be lovable" as cover for not shipping.
- **Lovable without minimum.** A 3-year project that "would be lovable when it ships" rarely ships.
- **Single user love.** Lovable for one person doesn't generalize.

### When MVP is still right
- **Genuinely novel markets** where the thesis itself is the question.
- **Internal / B2B tools** where polish matters less than function.
- **Pivots / explorations** where the thing being tested is the idea, not the execution.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI tooling makes MLP easier to ship.** Polished prototypes from vibe-coding tools meet the love bar at minimum scope better than pre-AI MVPs.
- **Cf. [vibe-coding](vibe-coding.md), [ai-prototyping](ai-prototyping.md)**.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a designer**: MLP is your moment. Push for it over MVP when stakes warrant.
- **If you're a CEO contemplating "should we ship the rough version?"**: ask if it would be lovable. If not, scope-cut to something that is.
- **If you're an investor**: products that ship MLP often have stronger early retention than MVP-shipped competitors.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2023**: Jiaona Zhang on Lenny's Podcast — corpus introduction of MLP framing.
- **2024-2026**: AI-tooling makes MLP shipping more accessible.
