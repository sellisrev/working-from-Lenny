# Product strategy frameworks

## Current consensus

The corpus has converged on a small number of high-leverage product strategy frameworks. Use them as a stack, not in competition.

### The strategy stack (Ravi Mehta's framing)

Mission → Vision → Strategy → Goals → Roadmap → Task. Each level constrains the next. PMs who can't connect a roadmap item back through goals → strategy → vision → mission are operating on incomplete strategy. ([Lenny's Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_yQMGHHl49g), 2025-09-29)

### Roger Martin's 5 questions

Cf. [evaluating-product-bets](evaluating-product-bets.md), [assessing-product-strategy-as-board](assessing-product-strategy-as-board.md). Winning aspiration, where to play, how to win, capabilities, management systems. The canonical strategy diagnostic.

### Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers

Concrete moat taxonomy: scale economies, network effects, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, process power. For strategy assessment: which of these are you building? "Better product" is not a power.

### Rumelt's Good Strategy / Bad Strategy

Diagnosis + guiding policy + coherent action. Bad strategy is "fluff" (motherhood goals, slogans). The most useful diagnostic for separating real strategy from aspirational language.

### Strategy Blocks (corpus 2025)

A more recent corpus addition — operator's guide to strategy as composable building blocks rather than top-down monolithic plans. Useful for teams executing within a larger strategy.

### Foundation Sprint (Knapp & Zeratsky, 2024)

For very early-stage strategy formation: a structured 2-day version of the broader strategy work. Cf. [foundation-sprint](foundation-sprint.md). Most useful for pre-PMF startups crystallizing their first version of all the above.

## What's changed in 2025-2026

Three updates to traditional strategy thinking:

1. **Distribution as the primary moat.** Evan Spiegel ([Lenny's Podcast 2026-04-26](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-distribution-has-become-the-most-important-moat)): "distribution has become the most important moat." For 2026 strategy, treat distribution as one of the load-bearing capabilities (Martin question 4) and one of the candidate powers (Helmer's branding / network effects / cornered resource).

2. **AI-readiness as a strategy dimension.** Cf. [ai-org-restructuring](ai-org-restructuring.md). Howie Liu's "if we were founding AI-native today, what would we do?" question is now a standard strategy diagnostic. The gap between current strategy and the AI-native answer is your strategic AI debt.

3. **Iteration rate is now part of strategy.** Cat Wu / Anthropic: "ship in research preview within 1-2 weeks." Strategies that depend on quarterly cycles for product iteration lose to strategies that ship weekly. This is operational, not strategic — but the distinction has blurred.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a founder doing strategy formulation**: start with Foundation Sprint or Roger Martin's 5 questions, not with a roadmap. Strategy → goals → roadmap, not the reverse.
- **If you're an exec reviewing strategy at a portfolio / division level**: the Helmer + Rumelt screen catches most strategy quality issues. Roger Martin's 5 questions is the diagnostic deeper-dive.
- **If you're a board member at a Series B+**: the AI-readiness question is your high-leverage move. Companies still strategizing as if AI is a feature line item are mis-positioned for the next 24 months.
- **If you're a CFO or COO**: the management-systems question (Martin #5) is yours. Strategy without enabling systems decays predictably.
- **If you're a non-PM hearing "this is our product strategy"**: ask for the strategy stack. If they can't show goal → strategy → vision linkage, the thing they're calling strategy is closer to a roadmap.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2017-2021**: corpus accumulates strategy-doc pieces. Marty Cagan, Roger Martin, Geoffrey Moore canon established.
- **2023-2024**: Helmer (7 Powers), Rumelt (Good Strategy Bad Strategy) episodes formalize the moat / structure / diagnosis vocabulary.
- **2024-07-25**: Roger Martin episode — 5 essential questions becomes the default board-meeting frame.
- **2024-2025**: Knapp & Zeratsky Foundation Sprint provides structured early-stage strategy formation method.
- **2025-2026**: AI-era updates layer on top of (not replacing) the canon. Distribution-as-moat (Spiegel), iteration-rate-matters (Cat Wu), AI-readiness-diagnostic (Howie Liu) all 2025-2026 additions.

## Open questions

- Is "distribution as primary moat" durable or specific to the 2024-2026 AI-tooling commoditization moment? Spiegel argues durable; Helmer's framework would absorb it as branding / network effects / cornered resource depending on the case.
- How do you structure strategy meetings with a 1-2 week iteration cycle? Pre-AI strategy was set quarterly; AI-native ships features weekly. The quarterly review still has a role for direction-setting, but the relationship is unsettled.
- For very-large-org strategy (Fortune 500 scale): does the AI-readiness diagnostic apply, or are these companies operationally locked into their pre-AI strategy?
