# Building product roadmaps (authoring)

## Current consensus

For the stakeholder-consumer view, see [reading-product-roadmaps-as-stakeholder](reading-product-roadmaps-as-stakeholder.md). This is the PM-author view.

### Janna Bastow's themes-not-Gantt framing (canonical)
- **Now / Next / Later** structure. Themes ordered roughly by priority; specific items within themes.
- **Dates only on near-term work.** Beyond 1-2 quarters, ranges; beyond that, themes.
- **Outcomes over outputs.** Theme = "increase activation by 20%"; specific items = candidate solutions.
- **Update continuously.** Roadmap is a living artifact, not a snapshot.

### One team, one roadmap (Lenny 2020)
- Multiple competing roadmaps signal organizational misalignment, not multiple valid views.
- The PM's job is consolidation: a single shared artifact that speaks to engineering, design, sales, support, exec.

### The Mission → Vision → Strategy → Goals → Roadmap → Task chain
Cf. [product-strategy](product-strategy.md), Ravi Mehta strategy stack. Roadmap must trace back to strategy; if it doesn't, the roadmap is reactive.

### What's in a great roadmap
- **Themes** (ordered by priority).
- **Within each theme**, specific candidate items and their hypotheses.
- **Dependencies** flagged (cross-team, capability-gated, AI-model-gated).
- **Confidence levels.** Items in next quarter usually 50-80% confidence; further-out items lower.
- **Explicit non-goals.** What you're not doing this quarter and why.

### Common authoring failure modes
- **Roadmap as wishlist.** Everything anyone wants, no prioritization.
- **Roadmap as commitment register.** Hard dates on fuzzy work; broken trust when slip.
- **Roadmap as feature catalog.** No outcome connection.
- **Hidden roadmaps.** Sales has their version; product has theirs; engineering has theirs. Cf. [reading-product-roadmaps-as-stakeholder](reading-product-roadmaps-as-stakeholder.md).

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Horizon compresses.** AI-native companies (Anthropic, Cursor, Lovable) work on weeks-to-month horizon for shipping items. Beyond that, themes.
- **AI capabilities as roadmap dependencies.** "We'll ship X when the model can do Y reliably (eval > 80%)."
- **Prototypes appear earlier in the roadmap.** Cf. [ai-prototyping](ai-prototyping.md). Some roadmap "items" are now interactive demos.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an exec or board member**: ask for the roadmap structure (themes / now-next-later) and confidence levels. A roadmap of just dated features hides risk.
- **If you're an engineer**: insist on dependency clarity. Roadmaps that don't show dependencies underestimate effort.
- **If you're a sales lead**: cf. [working-with-pms-as-sales](working-with-pms-as-sales.md). Bring deal data to roadmap conversations; expect themes-and-confidence answers, not date commitments.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2020**: Lenny "One team, one roadmap"; "Where great product roadmap ideas come from" — foundational.
- **2022-08**: Janna Bastow (Mind the Product / ProdPad) — themes-not-Gantt canon.
- **2025-2026**: AI-native horizon compression.
