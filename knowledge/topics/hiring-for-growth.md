# Hiring for growth roles

## Current consensus

Growth hiring has six durable rules (Brian Balfour / Lenny "Six rules of hiring for growth," 2022) plus 2025-2026 updates:

### Six durable rules
1. **Hire for the role you have, not the role you want to have.** Pre-PMF, hire for hands-on growth experimentation. Post-PMF, hire for scale operations.
2. **Function-specific specificity matters.** "Head of Growth" is a meaningless title; "Head of Growth-Marketing" or "Head of Growth-Product" are different jobs requiring different hires.
3. **Look for builders, not analysts-at-rest.** Growth requires shipping experiments, not just diagnosing data.
4. **Cross-functional empathy.** Growth touches eng, design, marketing, sales. Hire for someone who has played at least two of these.
5. **The "scrappy first growth hire" pattern.** Crystal Widjaja (Gojek): early growth hires should be scrappy, willing to do unscalable work.
6. **Avoid the agency-hire-as-employee mistake.** People with marketing-agency backgrounds can be brilliant but often don't transition to product-led growth contexts.

### When to hire your first growth team
Lenny's "How to hire your first growth team" (2024): roughly post-PMF, when you have a working acquisition channel that you want to scale and a working retention curve. Pre-PMF growth hires often fail because there's nothing to grow yet.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
7. **AI-fluency is now table stakes for growth hires.** A growth lead who can't use Cursor / Claude Code to ship landing-page experiments themselves is at a disadvantage. The "I'll request the change from engineering" model is outpaced by growth leads who ship their own changes.
8. **AEO + content + AI loops.** Cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md). Growth leads now need to think about answer-engine optimization, not just SEO. Hires from pre-2024 SEO backgrounds may bring decayed playbooks.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO making your first growth hire**: don't make this hire pre-PMF. Find PMF first; then hire for scale.
- **If you're a CFO or board member reviewing growth hiring**: ask about the specific growth model the team is hiring against. "Hiring a Head of Growth" without naming the model is a red flag.
- **If you're a marketing or sales lead**: growth roles increasingly overlap with both functions. Expect ambiguity; coordinate explicitly to avoid territory wars.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022**: Brian Balfour + Lenny six-rules canon.
- **2023-2024**: corpus expands with role-specific content: Head of Growth recruiting (Casey Winters / Harry Stebbings), growth team structures.
- **2025-2026**: AI-tooling expansion shifts growth hire profile toward builders.
