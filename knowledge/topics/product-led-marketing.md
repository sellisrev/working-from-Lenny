# Product-led marketing

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "Product-led marketing" (2022) + Emily Kramer (Asana, Carta, MKT1) + Hila Qu's PLG canon.

### What "product-led marketing" means
- **Marketing where the product itself is the marketing.** Free tier, generous trial, embedded virality.
- **The product surface is the brand surface.** Customer experience IS marketing.
- **Distinct from PLG.** PLG is the growth motion; PLM is how you market within that motion.

### Tactics
1. **Free / generous tier as TOFU.** Cf. [freemium-vs-trial](freemium-vs-trial.md). Sign-up is the lead.
2. **Product features as launchable moments.** Each feature ship is a marketing moment; cadence drives awareness.
3. **In-product education.** Help center, tutorials, embedded onboarding all do marketing work.
4. **Community as marketing channel.** Notion-style communities, Slack-style ecosystems.
5. **Customer artifacts as social proof.** Templates, case studies, user-generated content.

### Emily Kramer's framing (MKT1)
- **Marketing should serve the product strategy, not the other way around.**
- **Rich content libraries** — templates, examples, courses — are increasingly marketing's deliverable.
- **Brand and demand together**, not separated organizationally.

### Common failure modes
- **PLM without PLG.** Product-led marketing for a sales-led product is theater.
- **Free tier too generous.** Cf. [freemium-vs-trial](freemium-vs-trial.md), Equals case study. PLM only works if conversion economics support it.
- **Product launches without marketing layer.** Shipping features without making them marketing moments wastes them.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AEO + answer-engine citations** (cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md)) reward branded products with strong content libraries.
- **AI-generated viral artifacts** (Lovable, Cursor outputs) are a new PLM primitive.
- **Cf. [content-driven-growth](content-driven-growth.md)** — increasingly overlaps with PLM.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a marketing lead at a PLG company**: your product team is your team. Engineering features that drive marketing moments matter.
- **If you're a CEO**: PLM requires alignment between product, marketing, and growth. Don't silo them.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022**: Lenny "Product-led marketing" + Emily Kramer episode — corpus reference.
- **2024-2026**: AI-generated artifacts as new PLM primitive; AEO emerges as new channel.
