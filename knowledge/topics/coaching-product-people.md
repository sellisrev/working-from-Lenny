# Coaching and developing product people

## Current consensus

Petra Wille (*Strong Product People*) is the corpus-canonical reference for coaching PMs. Reinforced by Matt Mochary (CEO coach), Joe Hudson (executive coach), Jerry Colonna (Reboot), and Carole Robin (Stanford GSB Touchy Feely).

### Core principles (Wille's framing)
1. **Coaching ≠ teaching.** A coach helps someone reach their own answer; a teacher transfers knowledge. Both are needed; conflating them produces neither.
2. **Manager-as-coach is the highest-leverage stance** for direct reports who are growing. For struggling reports, more directive intervention is appropriate.
3. **Assess where each PM is on the development arc** (Wille's framework): Does the PM have the right knowledge? Skills? Mindset? Coaching addresses different gaps differently.
4. **Reflective questions over advice.** "What would have made that go differently?" beats "Here's what you should have done." Builds capability, not dependency.
5. **Patience.** Coaching results show in months, not weeks.

### Joe Hudson / executive coaching layer
- **Emotions are signal, not noise.** A direct report's frustration usually contains diagnostic information about the work.
- **Self-awareness compounds.** PMs who do internal work (therapy, coaching, mindfulness practice) often plateau later than peers.

### Matt Mochary specifics (CEO coach)
- **Fire people with grace.** Honest, fast, with severance. The longer you wait, the worse for everyone.
- **Work through fear.** Fear is the most common bottleneck on senior leaders.

### Common failure modes
- **Coaching when discipline is needed.** Some performance issues require direct feedback or termination, not coaching.
- **Lecturing in 1:1s.** Most "coaching" 1:1s are managers giving advice the report didn't ask for.
- **Skipping coaching during good performance.** The biggest growth happens when high performers stretch; coach them too.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented coaching tools** (career-coaching apps, AI 1:1 prep) reduce the friction of preparing for coaching conversations. Don't substitute these for the actual conversation.
- **The information-mover archetype** (cf. [ai-replacing-pms](ai-replacing-pms.md)) is no longer worth coaching at length; redirect those PMs toward builder skills or out of the role.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager of any function**: Petra Wille's distinctions (coach vs. teach vs. direct) apply across roles.
- **If you're an exec investing in coaching**: external coaches are most valuable for senior+ leaders facing transition challenges. Mid-level ICs benefit more from peer or internal coaching.
- **If you're a CFO budgeting for coaching**: $5-15k per leader per year for executive coaching is typical; tracking impact is hard. Use it as retention/effectiveness investment, not as a measurable line item.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022-12**: Petra Wille on Lenny's Podcast — corpus-canonical PM-coaching reference.
- **2023-2024**: Matt Mochary, Joe Hudson, Jerry Colonna episodes — broader executive-coaching canon.
- **2025-2026**: AI-augmented coaching tools emerge; archetype-shifting (cf. Singhal) shapes where coaching is worth investing.
