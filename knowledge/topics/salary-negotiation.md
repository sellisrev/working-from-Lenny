# Salary and comp negotiation

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "The 10 commandments of salary negotiation" (2021) + "The ultimate guide to negotiating your comp" (2025).

### The 10 commandments (Lenny 2021, durable)
1. **Don't reveal your number first.**
2. **Always counter.** Even an excellent offer.
3. **Negotiate the package, not just base salary.** Equity, signing bonus, RSUs, target bonus, leveling, start date, vacation.
4. **Use the BATNA (best alternative to a negotiated agreement).** Multiple offers strengthen everything.
5. **Take time to think.** Don't accept on the call.
6. **Be specific in counters.** "I'd be excited at $X" beats "more would be nice."
7. **Don't lie about competing offers.** Specific lies get checked.
8. **Negotiate level + scope, not just comp.** Same comp at higher level / scope is much better long-term.
9. **Know the bands.** Levels.fyi, glassdoor, candor.co, network conversations.
10. **It's not personal.** The recruiter expects negotiation; failing to negotiate often leaves money on the table.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI PM roles command premium comp.** AI-fluency is a comp lever. Cf. [pm-job-market](pm-job-market.md).
- **Remote vs in-office comp arbitrage** has narrowed; major companies now have geo-adjusted bands.
- **Builder PMs negotiate from strength.** Cf. Singhal: top of the market is hot; bottom is soft.
- **Founder-track alternatives strengthen the BATNA.** "I might just go found something" is increasingly credible and changes the negotiation.

### Common failure modes
- **Accepting first offer.** Almost always leaves money on the table.
- **Negotiating one variable.** Base salary alone often has ceiling; equity, signing, level have separate buckets.
- **Negotiating against yourself.** "What do I need to do to get the offer?" is a closing move; not a negotiation move.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a hiring manager**: candidates who negotiate well are usually higher-quality hires. Don't penalize them.
- **If you're a CEO designing comp bands**: be ready for top candidates to negotiate above-band on level + scope. Have an answer.
- **If you're a recruiter**: leveling + scope are usually the most leverage-able variables for a recruiter helping the team.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021-2022**: Lenny "10 commandments of salary negotiation" — foundational.
- **2025**: Lenny "The ultimate guide to negotiating your comp" — updated with current data.
- **2026**: AI-PM premium + builder-track market dynamics.
