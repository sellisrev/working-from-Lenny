# Parental leave coverage planning

## Current consensus

Lenny "How to create an exceptional coverage plan for your parental leave" (2024) is the corpus-canonical reference.

### What an exceptional coverage plan looks like
1. **Start planning 3-4 months ahead.** Not 3-4 weeks.
2. **Document your role at the level your stand-in needs.** Workflows, decisions, contacts, vocabulary.
3. **Pre-identify decision-makers for predictable issues.** Who decides X while you're out? Make explicit.
4. **Communicate transition to stakeholders.** Customers, peers, leadership.
5. **Don't be reachable.** Real disconnection makes the leave actually restorative.
6. **Plan re-onboarding.** First 2-4 weeks back are catch-up, not full pace.

### Why most leaves are mismanaged
- **Founders / senior people don't plan early enough.**
- **Teams don't surface what only the leaver knew.**
- **Cultural pressure to be "available."** Slack just-in-case messaging breaks the leave.
- **Re-onboarding skipped.** Returning at full pace burns out the returner.

### The legal + benefits backdrop
- **US is regressive globally on parental leave.** 0-12 weeks paid is typical.
- **Tech companies often offer 12-26 weeks.** Better than US average; below European norms.
- **Padding company leave with PTO + short-term-disability** stretches it. Plan ahead.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Remote work + AI tools** make some coverage easier (recurring AI-summaries of meeting notes, automated status updates).
- **AI-augmented role documentation.** "Document my role for someone covering" can be partly drafted by AI, then revised.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager whose report is going on leave**: support the plan; protect their disconnection.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: make leave planning + re-onboarding part of the company's standard practice.
- **If you're an exec**: model taking leave fully if it applies. Cf. [taking-time-off](taking-time-off.md).

## How thinking has evolved

- **2024**: Lenny "How to create an exceptional coverage plan" — corpus foundational.
- **2025-2026**: AI tooling assists role documentation + status async during leave.
