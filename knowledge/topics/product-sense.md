# Developing product sense

## Current consensus

Product sense is the trained intuition for what users will love, what will work, and what to cut — combining customer empathy, taste, judgment, and pattern-matching from many shipped products.

The corpus consensus on how to develop it:

1. **Use products. A lot.** Every PM in the corpus who has been asked says the same: spend hours daily using products in your category and adjacent ones. Notice what works, what frustrates, why.
2. **Ship things, not specs.** You build product sense by shipping, watching reactions, and updating priors. PMs who never ship don't develop product sense; they develop product-document sense.
3. **Talk to users constantly.** Marty Cagan, Teresa Torres, Bob Moesta all converge: sustained customer-discovery practice is the input to product sense. Cagan's empowered teams talk to 5+ users per week.
4. **Study the great products and the great failures.** Lenny / Brandon Chu both recommend deep-dives on company case studies. Tom Conrad's "Billion dollar failures and successes" is a highlight.
5. **For 2026 AI products specifically: develop AI product sense.** Tal Raviv & Aman Khan (Feb 2026): use Cursor for daily non-technical work; spend 2-3 months in AI coding agents; build the muscle through hands-on use, not video courses. Cf. [ai-pm-skills](ai-pm-skills.md).

The "definitive guide to mastering product sense interviews" piece (Lenny, 2025) suggests product sense is also a learnable interview skill: structured frameworks for breaking down product critique questions, dimensions to consider (user need, business case, design quality, edge cases, risks).

## What's changed in 2025-2026

- **AI product sense is a distinct skill.** Pre-AI product sense plus AI-product-sense are not interchangeable. Tal & Aman's framing — coding agents make AI's reasoning visible in a way ChatGPT chat windows don't — is the canonical 2026 prescription.
- **The "use products" prescription expands to include using AI tools as products and as products-being-built.** Living in Cursor / Claude Code is a 2026 product-sense practice that didn't exist in 2022.
- **Product taste is the new vocabulary.** Cat Wu (April 2026): product taste is the rarest skill at Anthropic and the hire-anyone-who-has-it bar. "Sense" and "taste" overlap heavily; "taste" emphasizes the active selection / refusal angle.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a designer wondering whether you can move into PM**: design background plus product sense practice maps cleanly. Use products, ship things, talk to users — the practices are familiar.
- **If you're an engineer wondering same**: same. Cat Wu (Anthropic) explicitly hires "engineers with great product taste" for PM-shaped roles.
- **If you're a hiring manager**: ask for shipped product examples in PM interviews, not framework-recitation. Cf. [hiring-pms-as-non-pm](hiring-pms-as-non-pm.md).
- **If you're an exec hiring a head of product**: their product sense should manifest as pointed product decisions you respect — even if you'd have decided differently. A head of product who can't articulate strong product opinions doesn't have product sense yet.
