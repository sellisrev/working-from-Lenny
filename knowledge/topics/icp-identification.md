# Identifying ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Current consensus

Lenny "How to identify your ideal customer profile (ICP)" (2024) is the corpus-canonical reference.

### What an ICP is
- **The customer segment** that gets the most value from your product, fastest, with the lowest churn.
- Not who you "want" to sell to; who you should sell to based on data.
- Specific firmographic + behavioral attributes (B2B): industry, size, role, current solution, urgency.

### Why ICP matters
- **Resource allocation.** Sales, marketing, product all benefit from focused targeting.
- **Faster deals.** ICP customers close 2-5x faster than non-ICP.
- **Better retention.** ICP customers churn less. Cf. [retention](retention.md).
- **Reference flywheel.** Concentrated ICP customers refer similar customers.

### How to identify your ICP (Lenny canonical)
1. **Look at your highest-LTV customers.** Common attributes?
2. **Look at your lowest-churn customers.** Same?
3. **Ask sales who closes fastest.** The "easy wins" reveal ICP fit.
4. **Test sharper segmentation.** Marketing campaigns to specific sub-segments validate or refute hypothesis.
5. **Document and re-test annually.** ICP shifts with product and market.

### Common failure modes
- **Too broad.** "B2B SaaS companies" isn't an ICP; "mid-market hospitality HR managers at 200-500 employee companies" might be.
- **Confusing buyer with user.** Both matter; both should be in the ICP.
- **Letting sales pull in non-ICP deals.** Short-term revenue cost; long-term focus damage.
- **Treating ICP as static.** Markets shift; revisit.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented data** can identify ICP patterns faster (LTV cohort analysis, churn pattern analysis).
- **AI products often have unusual ICPs.** Early adopters skew technical; mainstream ICP is different.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO**: ICP is a leadership decision; don't delegate it.
- **If you're a CMO**: messaging should target the ICP; broader reach often dilutes value.
- **If you're a VP Sales**: pipeline ICP fit matters more than pipeline volume.
- **If you're a product lead**: roadmap should serve the ICP; non-ICP feature requests usually deferred.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2024-08**: Lenny "How to identify your ideal customer profile (ICP)" — corpus foundational.
- **2025-2026**: AI-augmented analysis enables faster / sharper ICP identification.
