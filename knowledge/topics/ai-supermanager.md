# Becoming an AI-augmented supermanager

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "How to become a supermanager with AI" (2024) + "Make product management fun again with AI agents" (2024) + Hilary Gridley (Whoop, How I AI episodes) practical workflow content.

### What a "supermanager" is
A manager who uses AI tooling to amplify their effectiveness 2-5x, freeing time for the human work (coaching, strategy, relationship-building) that only managers can do.

### Concrete patterns
1. **AI-drafted communications.** 1:1 prep notes, performance review drafts, status updates, board-meeting-prep.
2. **AI-summarized data.** Customer feedback synthesis, team activity summaries, competitive landscape monitoring.
3. **AI-assisted decision frameworks.** Pre-mortem checklists, tradeoff analyses, calibrated decision logs.
4. **AI-facilitated learning loops.** Personal AI coaches; retrospective analysis; pattern-recognition across past decisions.

### What the AI-augmented manager doesn't outsource
- **The actual coaching conversations.** AI can draft prep; the human conversation is still human.
- **The trust-building work.** Relationships are not delegable.
- **The hard decisions.** AI can structure tradeoffs; the call is still yours.
- **The narrative work.** Vision, conviction, leadership voice remain human.

### Hilary Gridley's How I AI episodes
- **Automation patterns** for recurring tasks (newsletter prep, decision logs, calendar prep).
- **Personalized agents** (cf. [ai-second-brain](ai-second-brain.md)) for specific recurring workflows.
- **The bottleneck shift**: routine work is automated; the limit becomes how much human work you can do well.

### Common failure modes
- **AI-drafted communications shipped without revision.** The reader can tell.
- **Outsourcing the coaching.** "Let me ask Claude how to handle this 1:1" instead of doing the work.
- **Tool fetish.** Adding 30 AI tools without integrating any deeply.
- **Forgetting the human work.** Most management value is in the relationships; AI amplifies but doesn't substitute.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Supermanager-as-norm**, not differentiation. Most senior leaders are now AI-augmented.
- **The bar for non-AI-augmented managers is dropping.** A 2026 senior manager who doesn't use AI tools is at a competitive disadvantage.
- **Cf. [ai-pm-skills](ai-pm-skills.md), [ai-replacing-pms](ai-replacing-pms.md)**: the same shift applies to managers as to ICs.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager not yet using AI in your management practice**: the gap is large. Start with one practice (drafted 1:1 prep, weekly retro structure) and expand.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: training senior leaders on AI-augmented practices is high-leverage. Many have not adapted.
- **If you're an exec setting expectations for direct reports**: ask what AI practices they're using. Reports who've built supermanager workflows perform measurably better.
