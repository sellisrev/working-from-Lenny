# Preparing for layoffs

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "How to be prepared for layoffs" (2023) + Phyl Terry (*Never Search Alone*, 2024) + Singhal v2 (2026) on the upcoming shed-and-rehire wave.

### What being "prepared" actually means
1. **Financial runway.** 6-12 months of expenses saved removes immediate panic if it happens.
2. **Active network maintenance.** Stay in touch with people quarterly, not just when you need a job.
3. **Documented portfolio.** Shipping wins, decisions made, impact metrics — keep these current; not just your LinkedIn.
4. **Skill currency.** AI-fluency in 2026 is non-negotiable. Builder PMs are getting rehired; information-mover PMs aren't.
5. **Diversified perspective.** Side projects, advisory roles, networking outside your company. Cf. Phyl Terry "never search alone."

### Phyl Terry's "Never Search Alone" framing
- **Don't job-search alone.** Form a small group (3-5 people) who help each other through transitions.
- **Document what you actually want** before the panic of unemployment narrows your choices.
- **Pace yourself.** Job searches at desperate speed produce bad outcomes.

### What to do during layoffs (if you're affected)
- **Take the severance package.** Don't negotiate severance to zero; it doesn't help you.
- **Use COBRA / health insurance carefully.** Plan transition to avoid coverage gaps.
- **Reach out widely.** Most jobs come through warm connections; this is when your network maintenance pays off.
- **Don't rush to apply.** First 2 weeks: sit with what just happened. After: structured search.

### What to do during layoffs (if you're not affected)
- **Don't survivor's-guilt yourself into longer hours.** Cf. [burnout-and-resilience](burnout-and-resilience.md).
- **Stay connected to ex-colleagues.** Their next role may matter for yours.
- **Recalibrate your career trust.** If your company laid off, the implicit "we're all in this together" contract is renegotiated.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Singhal v2 forecast (April 2026)**: 30k shed / 8k AI-first hire dynamics ahead at major tech companies.
- **AI-native skills are the differentiation.** Builder PMs / engineers with AI-tooling fluency get rehired faster.
- **The "PM is dead" rhetoric** (cf. [`../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md`](../cautions/ai-replacing-pms.md)) is wrong on aggregate but right on archetype: information-mover PMs are at the highest layoff risk.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an engineer / designer / IC**: same playbook applies. Skill currency + network + portfolio + financial runway.
- **If you're a manager about to do layoffs**: read Matt Mochary on "fire people with grace"; severance + dignity matter; you'll be hiring some of these people again.
- **If you're a CEO contemplating layoffs**: do them once, decisively. Multiple rounds of small layoffs destroys trust faster than one large round.
