# Lessons from angel investing

## Current consensus

Lenny "Lessons from 140+ angel investments" (2021) + "Top angel investors in the U.S." (2024) + Andrew Wilkinson (75+ businesses) episode anchor the corpus consensus.

### The math reality (Lenny's data)
- **Most angel investments fail.** Power-law distribution: most go to zero; a few return everything.
- **Diversification matters.** 30+ investments is a sensible minimum to ride the power law.
- **Check sizes**: typically $5-25k early, $25-100k for later angels with capital.
- **Returns are long.** 7-10 years typical; some take 15+.

### What works
- **Invest in people, not ideas.** Founder quality dominates at the angel stage; the idea is too early to bet on.
- **Pattern-match on traits, not background.** Resilience, learning rate, judgment — not pedigree alone.
- **Add value, not just money.** Customer intros, hiring help, advice, network access.
- **Reserve for follow-ons.** Don't burn your capital on first checks; reserve to defend pro-rata in the winners.

### What doesn't work
- **Investing in friends without diligence.** Friendship doesn't make a good investment.
- **Following hot deals into bad valuations.** Momentum FOMO produces bad outcomes.
- **Single-thesis concentration.** All-AI or all-marketplace is risky if the thesis breaks.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI angel theses dominate** in 2025-2026 venture activity. The AI infrastructure layer (Cursor, Anthropic, etc.) has produced outsized winners; AI application layer is still being shaken out.
- **Solo capital is more accessible** via SAFEs and online platforms. Threshold for becoming an angel has dropped.
- **Andrew Wilkinson's "you're chasing the wrong idea"** episode (2025-07): even at scale, picking ideas right is hard; most ideas are wrong.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're considering angel investing**: budget what you'd be okay losing. Don't put non-discretionary money into early-stage equity.
- **If you're a founder taking angel money**: angel quality varies enormously. Reference-check angels the way they reference-check you.
- **If you're an existing employee with stock options**: secondary sales of private stock are increasingly available; plan for diversification, not all-eggs-one-basket.
