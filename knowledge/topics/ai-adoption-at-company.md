# Accelerating AI adoption at your company

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "25 proven tactics to accelerate AI adoption at your company" (2026) + Brian Scanlan / Intercom case study + Cat Wu / Anthropic + Howie Liu / Airtable + Asha Sharma / Microsoft.

### What works (corpus consensus)
1. **Top-down + bottom-up combined.** CEO endorsement + ground-level empowerment together; either alone produces less adoption.
2. **Set ambitious throughput goals.** Brian Scanlan / Intercom: CTO-set "2x R&D throughput" goal drove team-wide adoption.
3. **Skills-and-hooks repository.** Codify common patterns; enforce quality through automation. Cf. [ai-developer-productivity](ai-developer-productivity.md).
4. **Telemetry-first measurement.** Hook AI tools into observability; measure adoption + quality + cost together.
5. **Generous tooling budget.** Don't bottleneck adoption with seat constraints. The productivity gains justify the bill.
6. **Time-off encouragement.** Brian Scanlan explicitly: people come back from vacation having explored AI tools and become 10x more capable.
7. **Give people skills, not just tools.** Curated example workflows + internal training > "here's a Claude API key, good luck."

### Tactical 2026 patterns from the corpus
- **AI-first hiring filters** (Cat Wu): hire engineers / designers / PMs who already use AI tools fluently.
- **Mandatory AI-tool experimentation time** in the workweek (some Lenny-corpus companies allocate 4-8 hours/week).
- **AI office hours / brown bags** for skill transfer between power-users and others.
- **Eval-driven AI feature development** as quality gate.

### Common adoption-failure modes
- **Tool fragmentation.** Many seats across many tools; few people expert at any. Pick a primary tool stack; commit.
- **Mandate without enablement.** "Use AI tools" without training produces frustration.
- **Cost panic too early.** Most AI tooling bills look scary at first; payback is real but takes 3-6 months.
- **Unauthentic leadership.** CEOs who don't personally use AI tools fail to drive cultural adoption. Cf. IC CEO pattern in [founder-mode](founder-mode.md), [ai-org-restructuring](ai-org-restructuring.md).
- **Pilot purgatory.** Indefinite pilots that never expand to production produce no real adoption.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **2025 was experimentation; 2026 is institutionalization.** The companies that committed in 2024-2025 are now seeing measurable productivity gains.
- **Cost discipline** is the post-2026 focus. After demonstrating productivity, companies optimize for the API bill.
- **Cf. cautions on Claude Code "100% of my code"** (cf. [`../cautions/100-percent-claude-code.md`](../cautions/100-percent-claude-code.md)) — generalizing AI-native company practices to non-AI-native contexts has limits.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO**: drive adoption from your own use first. CEOs who don't use AI tools personally produce slow adoption.
- **If you're a CTO / VP Eng**: instrument before you mandate. Telemetry-first lets you measure what works.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: AI-fluency in hiring rubric + training programs + community of practice for AI users.
- **If you're a CFO**: AI tooling cost grows fast in adoption phase. Budget for it; don't surprise CTOs by cutting mid-adoption.
