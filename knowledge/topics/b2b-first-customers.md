# Finding the first 10 B2B customers

## Current consensus

The first 10 B2B customers are won via concierge work, not scaled GTM. The corpus consensus:

1. **Founder-led, full-stop.** Until you have 10 customers, the founder sells. Pete Kazanjy's "Founding Sales" canon: founders develop the muscle that informs the eventual sales team's playbook.
2. **Network is the source of the first 3-5.** Personal network, investors' networks, advisors. Cold outbound is rarely the first-customer channel.
3. **Bend over backwards on the first deal.** Custom features, custom pricing, dedicated support — whatever it takes. The early customer's success is your case study.
4. **Pick the same segment for the first 10.** A diverse first 10 leaves you unable to claim PMF for any segment. Same shape of customer is the goal.
5. **The pitch evolves rapidly.** Lenny "How today's fastest-growing B2B startups turned their early users into paying customers" (2022) + "How to find and win your first 10 B2B customers" (2024): expect to rewrite the pitch deck after every 2-3 customer conversations.

### Common failure modes
- **Skipping concierge work.** "We'll automate this later" leads to misunderstanding the customer; manual onboarding teaches you the product.
- **Hiring AEs to find the first 10.** AEs need a working playbook; the founder has to build it.
- **Discounting too aggressively early.** A customer who only wants you because you're free isn't a real customer. Set price based on value; discount selectively.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-native B2B startups (Lovable, Cursor enterprise tier) reach 10+ customers faster** because the product self-demos so well. The principle holds; the timeline compresses.
- **Outbound efficiency from AI tooling** (research, personalization at scale) helps with cold outbound. Not a replacement for network, but a useful supplement.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a founder pre-10-customers**: stop hiring; stop building tooling; sell. The single highest-leverage activity is talking to prospective customers.
- **If you're an investor evaluating a Series A pitch**: who closed the first 10? If the answer is the founder, that's a positive signal.
- **If you're an early sales hire at a startup**: don't expect a playbook. Your job is to help the founder articulate one.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2020**: Lenny — How today's fastest growing B2B businesses found their first ten customers; foundational.
- **2023-2024**: corpus expands with founder-led sales canon (Pete Kazanjy).
- **2024-2025**: Jen Abel "Sell the alpha not the feature" — enterprise sales playbook for $1M to $10M ARR.
