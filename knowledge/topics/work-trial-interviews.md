# Work trials in interviews

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "Adding a work trial to your interview process" (2022) + Elena Verna 4.0 (2025) describing Lovable's work-trial practice + corpus founder hiring canon.

### What a work trial is
- **Paid short-term engagement** (1-5 days; sometimes 1-2 weeks) where a candidate does real work for the team.
- **Used as the final interview step** before extending offer.
- **Reveals fit better than interviews** for both sides.

### Why work trials work
- **Real work signals real ability.** Interviews test interview skill; work trials test work skill.
- **Cultural fit becomes visible.** Both directions: candidate sees the team; team sees the candidate in flow.
- **Reduces hiring mistakes.** Most expensive hiring mistakes are people who interviewed great but couldn't do the job. Work trials catch this.

### Common failure modes
- **Free work.** Don't use work trials as cheap labor. Pay competitively.
- **Too long.** 1-2 weeks should be the maximum. Asking for a month puts candidates with current jobs at unfair disadvantage.
- **Vague success criteria.** Define what "success" looks like before the trial; both sides need clarity.
- **Skipping for senior hires.** Senior candidates also benefit from work trials; many senior-hire failures could have been caught by them.

### Lovable example (Elena Verna 4.0, 2025)
- "We do a lot of trials for people. So trial work to see them in action." Paid; couple of days; explicit probationary period after hire too.
- "This company is not for everybody. The pace here is insane." Cultural-fit signal.

### Limitations
- **Not all candidates can take a week off.** Currently-employed candidates have constraints. Negotiate around this.
- **Trial work isn't the same as long-term work.** A great trial doesn't guarantee long-term fit; pacing differs.
- **Cultural-fit-via-trial can amplify hiring biases.** "Fit" can become code for "looks like us." Calibrate.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-tooling fluency** can be tested in trials directly. Asking "build me a prototype using Cursor" surfaces real ability.
- **More AI-native companies use trials** as a standard practice. The Lovable model is spreading.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a hiring manager**: consider work trials especially for senior or load-bearing roles. The cost (compensation for a week) is small vs. mishire cost.
- **If you're a candidate offered a work trial**: agree if you can; it's a positive signal about the company's hiring discipline.
- **If you're a recruiter/HR**: design the trial process carefully. Bad work-trial design produces worse signal than no work trial.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022-08**: Lenny "Adding a work trial to your interview process" — corpus foundational.
- **2025**: Elena Verna 4.0 / Lovable example — work trials as standard at AI-native companies.
- **2025-2026**: AI-tooling-fluency testing within trials.
