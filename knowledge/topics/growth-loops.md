# Growth loops and flywheels

## Current consensus

Growth loops (Brian Balfour's framing) have replaced funnels as the dominant model in the corpus. The consensus:

- **Loops compound; funnels leak.** A funnel turns acquisition into a one-shot conversion. A loop produces more acquisition from each conversion.
- **The four loop categories** (Reforge / Balfour canonical taxonomy): viral loops, content loops, paid acquisition loops, sales-led loops. Most companies have one dominant loop and 1-2 supporting ones.
- **The "magic loop"** (Lenny's recent framing): visualize your growth as a loop diagram, identify the load-bearing edges, optimize there.
- **The Atomic Network** (Andrew Chen, James Currier): the smallest viable network that demonstrates self-sustaining growth. Build for the atomic network first; expand later.
- **The hierarchy of engagement** (Sarah Tavel): users moving deeper into the product (from try → habit → core action → power user). Loops that move users up the hierarchy outperform.
- **The racecar growth framework** (Dan Hockenmaier + Lenny): sequential analogy — engine + wheels + driver. Useful for diagnosing what's broken in a growth motion.
- **Demand-driving-supply** (Lenny's "little-understood growth loop"): rare but powerful in some businesses (Booking, Airbnb at certain phases). When applicable, dominant.

## What's changed in 2025-2026

- **AI features as growth loops.** AI features that produce shareable artifacts (Lovable apps, Cursor projects, Bolt UIs) generate built-in viral loops via the artifacts themselves. The generation cost subsidy (cf. [ai-pricing](ai-pricing.md)) functions as a marketing investment.
- **Content + AI loop.** AI search / AEO (cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md)) reshapes content-driven growth: brand citations in answer engines become the new high-value acquisition.
- **Distribution as primary moat** (Spiegel). Cf. [distribution-as-moat](distribution-as-moat.md). When products are commoditized, distribution loops become the durable moat.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a marketing lead**: stop thinking funnels for acquisition. Map your loops first; optimize the load-bearing edge.
- **If you're an exec evaluating growth strategy**: ask "what's the dominant loop?" If the answer is "we do everything," there's no real growth strategy.
- **If you're a board member at a Series A+ company**: cohort retention + loop diagram together is the best diagnostic for "is growth durable?" Each on its own can mislead.
- **If you're a CMO**: budget allocation should follow loop economics, not channel familiarity. The channel that worked at $1M ARR may not be the loop that scales to $50M.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2017-2020**: Brian Balfour's Reforge content establishes loops vocabulary; corpus picks it up.
- **2020-2022**: Lenny accumulates loop variants (atomic network, hierarchy of engagement, racecar framework).
- **2023-2024**: Sarah Tavel, Dan Hockenmaier, Andrew Chen episodes deepen specific loop types.
- **2025-2026**: AI-era growth loops emerge — AI-generated content as content loop; AEO as distribution shift.
