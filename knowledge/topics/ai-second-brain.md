# Personal AI copilots and second brains

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "How to build your PM second brain with ChatGPT" (2024) + "Build your personal AI copilot" (2025) + Hilary Gridley How I AI episodes.

### What a "second brain" is
- **Persistent context** about you, your work, your priorities, your patterns.
- **Used across tools** (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor) so the AI remembers what matters.
- **Augments memory + decision-making** for recurring tasks.

### Common patterns
1. **Personal markdown library.** Decisions, principles, notes — fed into the AI's context.
2. **Custom GPTs / Projects / Claude Projects.** Reusable contexts for recurring use cases.
3. **Memory features.** ChatGPT memory + Claude Dreams (announced May 2026) automate context capture.
4. **Voice memos + transcription.** Wispr Flow / similar — capture thinking; AI processes.
5. **Calendar + meeting integration.** Granola, Fathom, Otter — meeting transcripts become context.

### Lenny's PM second brain pattern
- **Decisions log.** Every notable decision with context + outcome (when known).
- **People notes.** Customers, colleagues, recurring contacts — facts and history.
- **Priorities current state.** Updated regularly.
- **Personal frameworks.** How you make decisions, your values, what you're learning.

### What works
- **Use it daily.** A second brain that's not consulted produces no value.
- **Curate ruthlessly.** Junk content makes the AI worse, not better.
- **Update regularly.** Stale second brain produces stale answers.

### Common failure modes
- **Tool fragmentation.** 10 tools, none used deeply.
- **Privacy concerns ignored.** Personal AI copilots often have personal data; design accordingly.
- **Over-reliance.** AI second brain doesn't replace human judgment; augments it.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Memory features mature.** Claude Dreams (2026-05) writes memory automatically from session reviews; ChatGPT memory similar.
- **Cross-tool integration improves.** MCP-style integration lets multiple tools share context.
- **Cf. [ai-supermanager](ai-supermanager.md), [ai-pm-skills](ai-pm-skills.md), [vibe-coding](vibe-coding.md)**.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager**: the AI-augmented manager (cf. [ai-supermanager](ai-supermanager.md)) needs a personal AI second brain to operate effectively.
- **If you're an exec**: invest a few hours setting yours up; payback is large.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: think about company-supplied AI tools for personal use; many companies block them and lose the productivity benefit.
