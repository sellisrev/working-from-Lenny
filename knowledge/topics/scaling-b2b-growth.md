# Scaling B2B growth engine

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "Scaling your B2B growth engine" (2023, part 7 of B2B series) + Meltem Kuran Berkowitz (Deel) on hypergrowth + Ben Williams (Snyk) on PLG-juggernaut.

### The transition from $1M to $100M ARR
1. **Productize the founder-led playbook.** Cf. [founder-led-sales](founder-led-sales.md), [enterprise-sales-playbook](enterprise-sales-playbook.md). The first 10 customers were founder-led; the path to $100M requires repeatable systems.
2. **Hire ahead of the curve in some functions, behind in others.** Sales: ahead. Ops: usually behind. Calibrate.
3. **Multiple acquisition channels.** Pure-PLG or pure-sales-led both top out; hybrid is the dominant scale-up pattern.
4. **Customer success as durable lever.** Net dollar retention >100% (ideally 110-130%) becomes the financial health metric.
5. **Specialization in the GTM org.** AE → SDR + AE; AE → AE + Customer Success Manager; etc.

### Specific patterns from the corpus
- **Snyk (Ben Williams)**: PLG juggernaut; product-led signal drives sales overlay (PLS).
- **Deel (Meltem Kuran)**: hypergrowth via product-fit + GTM machine.
- **Stripe / Datadog / Twilio**: the bottom-up + sales overlay canon.
- **Ramp (Geoff Charles)**: velocity + culture as scaling enabler.

### Common failure modes
- **Hiring sales too far ahead.** Cf. [enterprise-sales-playbook](enterprise-sales-playbook.md). 5+ AEs at $1M ARR usually fails.
- **Premature specialization.** Splitting roles before there's enough volume to specialize.
- **Lost focus on ICP.** Cf. [icp-identification](icp-identification.md). Scaling sometimes pulls in non-ICP customers; long-term cost.
- **Pricing not iterating with stage.** Pricing that worked at $1M often needs to change at $10M and again at $100M.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented GTM operations** are mainstream at scale-up B2B (auto-research, AI-generated outbound, AI-summarized customer signals).
- **Outcome-based pricing variants** (cf. [ai-pricing](ai-pricing.md)) for AI products require new GTM patterns.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO scaling B2B**: read Lenny's full B2B series; sequencing matters.
- **If you're a CFO**: NDR is the durable metric for growth-stage B2B; track it religiously.
- **If you're an investor**: evaluate scaling readiness, not just current metrics.
