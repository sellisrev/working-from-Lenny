# Performance reviews

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "The Power of Performance Reviews" (2018) + Camille Fournier (*The Manager's Path*) + Kim Scott (*Radical Candor*) corpus content.

### What good performance reviews do
1. **No surprises.** Anything in the review should already have been said in 1:1s. Surprise = manager failure.
2. **Specific, evidence-based.** Concrete examples; not "good attitude."
3. **Forward-looking.** Past performance is context; the review is about what's next.
4. **Calibrated across team.** "Top performer" means something specific relative to peers.
5. **Documented.** Both for the report's reference and for HR / promotion discussions.

### What good performance reviews don't do
- **Spring surprises.** If you're firing someone in the review, you waited too long.
- **Re-litigate.** Performance reviews aren't the place to re-fight earlier disagreements.
- **Compensate for management gaps.** A review doesn't fix bad coaching during the period.
- **Substitute for feedback.** Quarterly reviews aren't enough; ongoing feedback is.

### Best practices (corpus)
- **Self-review first.** Report writes their own assessment; manager responds. Reveals gaps in mutual understanding.
- **360 feedback selectively.** Useful for senior employees; overhead-heavy for junior.
- **Forward-looking goals.** Concrete what-changes for next period.
- **Calibration meetings across managers.** Reduces individual-manager bias.
- **Decouple comp from review somewhat.** Tightly coupling means reviews become comp negotiation; degrades the developmental conversation.

### Common failure modes
- **Compliance-only reviews.** Filling out forms without genuine engagement.
- **Stack-ranking.** Forced curves often produce unfair outcomes; corpus generally critical of strict stack-ranking.
- **Review-as-punishment.** Saving criticism for review time produces shock + defensiveness.
- **Manager defaulting to "Meets Expectations".** Lazy ratings degrade calibration.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented review drafting.** Tools can produce first-draft reviews from 1:1 notes + project records. Don't ship the AI draft; revise heavily.
- **Continuous-feedback platforms** (15Five, Lattice, etc.) with AI summarization reduce the "save it for review" pattern.
- **AI-fluency** as a 2026 review dimension: "is this person operating at the AI-augmented bar?"

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager**: invest in 1:1 quality so reviews aren't surprising.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: calibration meetings are high-leverage; design them deliberately.
- **If you're an IC**: write your self-review carefully. It anchors the conversation.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2018**: Lenny "The Power of Performance Reviews" — early corpus reference.
- **2024**: Camille Fournier on management; Kim Scott Radical Candor.
- **2025-2026**: AI-augmented review drafting; continuous-feedback platforms.
