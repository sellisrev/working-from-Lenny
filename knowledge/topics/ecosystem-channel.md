# Ecosystem as a growth channel

## Current consensus

Lenny "Ecosystem is the next big growth channel" (2025) is the corpus-canonical recent treatment.

### What "ecosystem" means as a growth channel
- **Being part of a platform's expansion** — appearing in marketplaces, integrations, app stores.
- **Building integrations into adjacent products** — your product appears in their workflow, theirs in yours.
- **Network position in the ecosystem** — being the canonical Slack integration for X, the canonical Salesforce integration for Y.

### Why ecosystem channels matter
- **Distribution leverage.** Your product reaches users via the ecosystem partner's distribution.
- **Lower CAC than paid.** Ecosystem-driven acquisition is often the cheapest channel.
- **Defensibility.** Deep integration produces switching costs (cf. [seven-powers](seven-powers.md)).
- **Co-marketing.** Ecosystem partners have incentive to promote you.

### What works
1. **Pick ecosystem partners with significant distribution.** Salesforce, Microsoft, Google, AWS, Slack at the platform tier.
2. **Build deep integration, not surface integration.** "Connect" is table stakes; "transforms how the user uses both products" is the win.
3. **Be early.** Most platform marketplaces feature the first 3-5 quality integrations heavily; later entrants compete for attention.
4. **Co-marketing.** Joint webinars, joint customer references, joint content beat announcement-and-walk-away.

### Common failure modes
- **Token integrations.** Adding a "Connect to Slack" button without Slack-specific value is checkbox marketing, not ecosystem strategy.
- **Platform dependency without value capture.** If 80% of your traffic comes from one ecosystem partner, they own you.
- **Integration without distribution.** Building integrations that no one in the ecosystem promotes is wasted engineering.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI ecosystems are emerging.** ChatGPT apps (Lenny "ChatGPT apps are about to be the next big distribution channel") are the new platform layer.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** as ecosystem standard — Anthropic's MCP creates a platform pattern for AI integrations.
- **Distribution-as-moat (Spiegel)** + ecosystem channel are increasingly intertwined strategies.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO/founder**: ecosystem investment is multi-year. Don't expect quick wins; do expect compounding distribution if you commit.
- **If you're a CMO**: ecosystem-driven leads + content + co-marketing produce the best B2B funnels in many categories.
- **If you're an engineer building integrations**: depth matters more than breadth. One excellent Slack integration beats five mediocre ones across platforms.
- **If you're an investor**: ecosystem-position-based moats are durable when present; ask about platform dependencies and value capture.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2025**: Lenny "Ecosystem is the next big growth channel" — corpus introduction.
- **2025-2026**: ChatGPT apps + MCP emerge as the new ecosystem layer.
