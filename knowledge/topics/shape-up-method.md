# Shape Up planning method (Ryan Singer / 37signals)

## Current consensus

Shape Up is Ryan Singer's planning + execution method developed at 37signals, later extracted as a book and an alternative to Scrum / Agile / continuous-roadmap models.

### The core framework
1. **Shaping**: pre-execution work to define a project to a specific level of detail (rough enough to leave room; specific enough to commit).
2. **Betting**: at the betting table, leadership commits to a specific shaped project for a fixed duration ("appetite").
3. **Building**: a small team (1 designer + 2 engineers typical) executes within the appetite.
4. **Cool-down**: 2-week breaks between cycles for housekeeping, exploration, fixing.

### Cycle length
- **6-week cycles** (Shape Up canonical) for most work.
- **2-week cycles** for smaller bets.
- **Cool-down** (2 weeks) between cycles.

### Why teams adopt Shape Up
- **Avoids scope creep through fixed appetite.** "If it doesn't fit in 6 weeks, we ship what fits and stop."
- **Avoids endless backlog.** Each cycle starts fresh; old "items" don't accumulate forever.
- **Embraces scope cuts.** Cuts are normal, not failure.

### Why teams don't adopt Shape Up
- **Doesn't fit calendar quarters.** OKR-driven companies often clash.
- **Requires shaping discipline upfront.** Many orgs don't have shapers (the senior PMs / designers who can do this).
- **Less useful for very-young teams.** Pre-PMF startups often need faster iteration than 6-week cycles.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI tooling compresses cycles.** A 6-week Shape Up cycle of 2020 might be a 2-3 week cycle in 2026 with the same scope.
- **Cycle duration vs research-preview cadence.** Cat Wu / Anthropic: 1-2 week research previews. Shape Up structure adapts but the canonical 6 weeks is decreasingly common.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an engineer who hates Scrum stand-ups**: Shape Up is the most credible alternative for product engineering. Worth reading.
- **If you're a CEO at a small company**: Shape Up's bias toward fixed appetite + commit / cut tradeoffs maps cleanly to startup velocity needs.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: Shape Up's "shapers / builders" distinction has implications for how you describe roles. A shaper is closer to a senior PM / staff engineer; a builder is a generalist engineer-PM.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2019**: 37signals publishes *Shape Up* book.
- **2024-04**: Ryan Singer on Lenny's Podcast — "A better way to plan, build, and ship products."
- **2025-2026**: AI-tooling compresses cycle lengths; canonical 6 weeks is now contextual.
