# Authentic leadership and curiosity

## Current consensus

Anchored in Ami Vora ("Making an impact through authenticity and curiosity", CPO at Faire, 2024) + adjacent corpus content (Carolyn Robin, Jerry Colonna, Joe Hudson).

### What "authentic leadership" means in this corpus
- **Showing up as yourself** at work, not as a corporate persona.
- **Genuine curiosity** about people, problems, and your own behavior.
- **Naming your reality** — including doubts, struggles, mistakes — rather than performing certainty.

### Why authenticity matters operationally
- **Trust accelerates.** Reports trust authentic leaders faster than performative ones.
- **Information flows better.** When the leader is real, reports share real concerns earlier.
- **Decision quality improves.** Leaders who acknowledge what they don't know calibrate better.

### Ami Vora's specifics
- **Bring your full self to work.** Suppression has a cost.
- **Curiosity over judgment.** "Why might this person be doing this?" beats "this person is wrong."
- **Lead with questions.** Authentic leaders make space for input rather than monologuing.

### Common failure modes
- **Confusing authenticity with oversharing.** Authentic doesn't mean unprofessional.
- **Performative authenticity.** "I'm being so honest right now" usually isn't.
- **Authenticity as excuse for unkindness.** "I'm just being real" can mask cruelty.

### How this connects to other corpus frameworks
- **Cf. [empathy-in-leadership](empathy-in-leadership.md), [conscious-leadership](conscious-leadership.md)**: overlapping framings emphasizing self-awareness.
- **Counter to wartime-CEO / no-days-off framings** (cf. cautions on Keith Rabois, Matt MacInnis).

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **Authenticity in 2026 increasingly includes being honest about AI uncertainty.** Leaders who pretend to know AI futures lose credibility; leaders who acknowledge their bets are uncertain build trust.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a manager**: practice noticing when you're performing vs. being real. The shift is internal; the impact is visible to reports.
- **If you're an exec coaching a peer**: authenticity isn't taught directly; usually emerges through self-awareness work.
- **If you're an HR / People leader**: leaders who can't be authentic have a coaching gap, not necessarily a performance gap.
