# Product design craft (Apple, Pinterest, Stripe lineage)

## Current consensus

Anchored in Bob Baxley ("35 years of product design wisdom from Apple, Disney, Pinterest", 2025-12), Katie Dill (Stripe Head of Design), Karri Saarinen (Linear — "Building with taste, craft, and focus"), Yuhki Yamashita (Figma).

### What design craft is (corpus consensus)
- **Obsession with the small things that compound.** Spacing, typography, interaction timing.
- **Editing more than adding.** Great design is what's removed; mediocre design accretes.
- **Coherence at the system level.** Individual screens that look great but don't fit together degrade the whole.
- **Refusal to ship before the bar is met.** Karri Saarinen / Linear — taste manifests as veto power.
- **Constant practice.** Bob Baxley's framing — design taste is built through 10,000+ hours of practice and feedback.

### Bob Baxley's specific points (2025)
- **Design is a continuous interrogation of "is this right?"** Not a deliverable.
- **Design taste develops via critique and exposure.** Not by reading; by doing and getting feedback.
- **Most product teams have insufficient design seniority.** Without a senior designer in the room, mediocre design ships.
- **Apple's secret was empowering designers, not orchestrating them.** Hierarchy + craft authority co-existed.

### Katie Dill / Stripe craft
- **Beautiful products through systemic investment in design.** Stripe spent on design when most B2B SaaS was treating it as decoration.
- **Design partnership with PM and engineering.** Cf. [working-with-pms-as-designer](working-with-pms-as-designer.md).

### Karri Saarinen / Linear craft
- **Building with taste means saying no to features that erode coherence.**
- **Velocity + taste co-exist.** Linear ships fast but maintains craft; the discipline is in the editing.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI tooling democratizes the visual layer.** v0 / Bolt / Lovable produce serviceable mocks; design craft remains in the editing and the system-level decisions.
- **The bar for B2B SaaS design has risen.** Stripe-quality design is now table stakes for venture-backed B2B; teams that under-invest fall behind.
- **Cf. [working-with-pms-as-designer](working-with-pms-as-designer.md)**: vibe-coded prototypes are not finished design; the role boundary requires explicit management.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO**: design quality is a multi-year cultural investment. Don't expect quick fixes.
- **If you're an engineer**: respect design craft; understand that "we should add X" or "let me change the spacing" is design work, not engineering work.
- **If you're a PM**: cultivate your design partner. Cf. [working-with-pms-as-designer](working-with-pms-as-designer.md).
- **If you're an investor**: design quality at seed/Series A predicts long-term brand strength. Companies with bad design at $1M ARR rarely catch up later.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2024-04**: Katie Dill on Lenny's Podcast.
- **2025**: Bob Baxley + Karri Saarinen + Yuhki Yamashita corpus design canon.
- **2025-2026**: AI-tooling democratizes visual; design craft moves toward system-level + editing.
