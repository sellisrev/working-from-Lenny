# Working with PMs as a sales / GTM lead

## Current consensus

The PM-Sales relationship has historically been the most fraught cross-functional pairing in the corpus. The 2026 working consensus:

- **Sales-led companies should hire sales before growth/product** (Elena Verna, 2022, [B2B growth episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bs15W2hOCDI)). Self-serve / PLG companies invert this.
- **Empowering product in a sales-driven org is a real challenge** (Lenny, "This Week #11" 2020-12). The default state is that sales pulls product around like a feature factory.
- **Founder-led sales until $300-500k ARR** (Lenny B2B series, 2023-10) — PMs aren't the bottleneck early; founders are. Bringing in PMs too early is a hire mistake.
- **The "sales-led PM" archetype** is a real and legitimate path: PMs whose primary input is sales conversations, who own roadmap items tied to deals, who push back on internal-engineering-driven priorities. This is not the "Feature Factory" pathology when it's done with discipline.
- **2026 update**: at AI-native B2B companies, the sales / product boundary is also blurring. AI features sometimes ship without sales involvement (research preview), and sales is increasingly expected to use the product to demo it (vibe-coded demos rather than slide decks).

## Five things sales / GTM leads want from PMs

1. **Roadmap clarity.** What's coming, when, with what likely impact on what deals? Sales needs to be able to make commitments. PMs who can't give a 90-day roadmap with confidence make sales' job impossible.
2. **A clear signal on whether to push back on a deal-blocking ask.** Some deals are worth bending the roadmap for; some are tail-wagging. PMs should be willing to say "yes, we'll do X" or "no, we won't, and here's why" — not "let me check."
3. **Pricing & packaging input on new features.** Cf. [ai-pricing](ai-pricing.md). PMs who hand new features to sales without pricing thought create immediate pipeline drag.
4. **Customer-research alignment.** When sales hears something repeatedly from prospects, it should reach the PM and influence the roadmap. PMs who treat sales feedback as low-quality noise are missing high-signal data.
5. **Willingness to demo their own product.** A PM who can't demo their feature in a sales call is a PM who hasn't dogfooded.

## What sales / GTM leads should bring to PMs

- **Pattern, not anecdote.** "We lost three deals this quarter on feature X" is signal. "One prospect didn't like color Y" is noise.
- **Willingness to be told no.** PMs who never say no are bad PMs (cf. [saying-no](saying-no.md)). Don't punish a PM for declining your ask if their reasoning is sound.
- **Pipeline data, not just pipeline assertions.** Numbers ground the conversation: deal size, weighted pipeline, churn risk specifically attributable to the feature gap.
- **Customer reference offers.** PMs need user research; sales has access to users. A 30-minute conversation with a prospect is more valuable to PM than 100 PM-led tickets.

## What this means for sales / GTM leads specifically

- **If you're a VP Sales or CRO at a SaaS company**: the relationship with the head of product is one of the highest-leverage relationships you have. Build trust in low-stakes interactions so high-stakes asks are believed.
- **If you're a founder doing founder-led sales**: don't hire a PM purely to translate between you and engineering. The "translator PM" is exactly the obsolete archetype. Either hire a builder PM who can drive product themselves, or wait.
- **If you're an AE in a sales-driven org**: the PM is not your enemy when they decline your roadmap ask. They may be the only person preventing the company from becoming a feature factory that loses to a more focused competitor.
- **If you're a customer-success lead**: your access to users is a high-value resource for the PM. Trade access for prioritization influence.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2020-12**: Lenny — "Empowering product in a sales-driven org" weekly Q&A. First corpus framing.
- **2021-2022**: Elena Verna 3.0 framework on sales-led vs PLG hiring sequence.
- **2023-10**: Lenny B2B series part 6 — founder-led sales until $300-500k ARR; first sales hire as a hungry senior AE.
- **2024-2025**: corpus expands B2B sales canon (Pete Kazanjy, Jen Abel, Sahil Mansuri).
- **2026-04**: Cat Wu — Anthropic ships features in research preview without heavy sales coordination; AI-native model.
