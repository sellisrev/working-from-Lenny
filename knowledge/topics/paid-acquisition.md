# Paid acquisition and performance marketing

## Current consensus

Anchored in Adam Grenier (Uber, MasterClass — "When and how to invest in new acquisition channels", 2022), Jonathan Becker (Thrive Digital — "Mastering paid growth", 2023), Timothy Davis (Shopify — "The ultimate guide to performance marketing", 2024), and Lenny "When and how to run a billboard campaign" (2024).

### When paid acquisition makes sense
1. **You have a working organic loop already.** Paid amplifies existing growth; it doesn't replace strategy.
2. **Unit economics support it.** LTV : CAC > 3:1 (3-month-ish payback period for SaaS; faster for consumer).
3. **You can attribute spend to outcomes.** Without attribution, paid is gambling.
4. **You have product-market fit at the targeted segment.** Paid acquisition pre-PMF burns capital fast.

### Channel-specific patterns (corpus references)
- **Meta + Google paid social/search** still dominate consumer paid. Saturated; CPM rising; targeting capability declining post-iOS-tracking-changes.
- **Connected TV** rising for premium consumer brands.
- **Billboards / out-of-home** make sense for brand-driven products in dense markets (Lenny piece) — measurable IF you control the assertion.
- **Influencer marketing** specific to category; less measurable.
- **Podcast advertising** rising for B2B-prosumer products.

### Adam Grenier's "when to invest in new channels" pattern
- **Existing channel hits saturation / CPM rises.** Time to test new channels.
- **Product / persona shift opens new channels.** New audience matches new channel.
- **Don't chase cool channels.** Test for fit; abandon fast if economics don't work.

### Common failure modes
- **Treating paid as primary growth without organic foundation.** Burns capital; rarely produces durable growth.
- **Optimizing for top-of-funnel attribution alone.** "Cost per signup" can decrease while "cost per paying customer" increases.
- **Channel lock-in.** Companies that skill up only on Meta + Google find themselves stuck when those channels saturate.
- **Ignoring brand.** Pure-performance-only marketing erodes brand over time.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-augmented creative production** dramatically reduces ad-creative cost. Teams can ship 100x more variants per dollar.
- **AI-targeting** improves matching but iOS/Android privacy changes have raised the difficulty bar.
- **AEO (cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md))** is partly replacing some of the SEO-paid budget mix.
- **Pre-roll / answer-engine sponsorship** are nascent paid categories.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CMO**: organic-first; paid as amplifier. Reverse this pattern usually fails.
- **If you're a CFO**: LTV : CAC ratios + payback period are the only durable paid-acquisition KPIs.
- **If you're a CEO at a venture-backed startup**: don't burn the round on paid before PMF. Most pre-PMF burn-rate disasters are paid acquisition mistakes.
