# Fundraising playbook (seed through growth)

## Current consensus

Lenny "A playbook for fundraising" (2021, foundational) + Jessica Livingston (YC) + Dalton Caldwell (YC) episodes.

### The structural pattern across rounds
1. **Pre-seed/Seed**: idea + team + early validation. ~$1-15M (varies wildly in 2026).
2. **Series A**: PMF evidence + initial growth + clear unit economics. $5-30M.
3. **Series B**: scaling motion proven + path to material revenue. $20-80M.
4. **Series C+**: optimization, geography expansion, product expansion. $50M+.

### Universal principles
- **Be in raising mode or not in raising mode.** Half-raising signals weakness; commit fully when you raise.
- **Multi-tracking.** Talk to many funds in parallel, not in series. Creates competitive pressure.
- **Lead first.** Once a lead is committed, follow-ons cascade. Without a lead, the round is theoretical.
- **The round is closed when wired.** Verbal commitments evaporate; signed term sheets can fall through. Don't celebrate prematurely.
- **Don't lie about competing offers.** Easy to verify; relationship-destroying when caught.

### Pitch-deck canonical structure
1. **Vision** (1 slide).
2. **Problem** (1-2 slides; specific, current, painful).
3. **Solution** (1-2 slides; how you address the problem).
4. **Why now** (1 slide; market inflection / Mike Maples).
5. **Traction** (1-2 slides; customers, revenue, growth).
6. **Business model** (1 slide).
7. **Team** (1 slide).
8. **Use of funds** (1 slide; specific, milestone-driven).

### Common failure modes
- **Spray-and-pray emails.** No personalization, no warm intro, no signal of fit.
- **Over-solving for valuation.** A high-valuation seed often hurts at Series A if metrics don't grow into the valuation.
- **Hidden bad news.** Investors find out anyway during diligence; surfacing it yourself is more credible.
- **Ignoring fit.** A misfit investor at high price is worse than a fit investor at fair price.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI rounds are faster.** Some AI seed rounds close in 2-4 weeks vs traditional 2-3 months.
- **AI is the dominant 2026 thesis.** Investors expect an AI angle even for non-AI-core companies.
- **Distribution as moat** is an increasingly credible pitch (Spiegel framing).

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO planning to raise**: timeline is the killer. Start fundraising 6 months before you need money; it always takes longer than expected.
- **If you're a CFO at a startup**: cash runway > 18 months at all times if possible; never raise from desperation.
- **If you're a board member**: helping with intros is one of the highest-leverage things you can do for your founder.
