# Consumer subscription growth and metrics

## Current consensus

Consumer subscription is its own subgenre with shape distinct from B2B SaaS. Corpus consensus:

### The subscription value loop (Lenny + Phil Carter framing)
Acquire → activate → retain → upsell / refer → reacquire-resurrect. Each step compounds; weak links throttle the whole loop.

### The most important consumer subscription metrics
- **Trial-to-paid conversion** (typical 5-25% depending on category)
- **Annual retention** (NOT monthly — consumer subs see month-1 cliffs masking year-1 reality)
- **LTV-to-CAC ratio** (3:1 minimum for venture-backed; lower is fine for bootstrapped niche)
- **Reactivation rate**: the under-discussed metric. Churned consumer subscribers often re-subscribe; track this and optimize for it.

### What works in consumer subscription
- **Habit-formation loops.** Duolingo streaks. The most-cited 2024-2025 mechanic in the corpus. Cf. [retention](retention.md).
- **Annual plans with monthly comparison anchoring.** "Save 30%" framing nudges users to annual.
- **Family / sharing tiers** that add stickiness (Netflix, Spotify, Apple One).
- **Content release cadence** for content-driven subs (Disney+, Lenny's Newsletter himself).

### What doesn't work or is risky
- **Indefinite free trials.** Users forget; charge them; refunds hurt. Time-bound trials with reminders work better.
- **"Cancel anytime" buried in the UX.** Short-term retention; long-term lawsuits and reputation damage.
- **Aggressive notification schemes.** Boost short-term DAU; tank long-term retention.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI features in consumer subs are pricing differentiators.** ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Perplexity Pro all justify $20/month tiers via AI capability. Some legacy consumer subs (entertainment, productivity) are adding AI to defend pricing.
- **The $200/month consumer tier is real now.** Anthropic Max, ChatGPT Pro, similar. Power users will pay this if the value is clear.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CFO at a consumer subscription company**: NDR is less applicable than for B2B; track gross dollar retention and reactivation rate together.
- **If you're a marketing lead**: the LTV horizon is years, not quarters. Don't optimize for first-month metrics at the cost of year-1 retention.
- **If you're an engineer building consumer subscription features**: cancel UX is a load-bearing UX surface. Make cancel easy AND make resubscribe easy.
