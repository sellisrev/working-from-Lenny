# Distribution as the most important moat

## Current consensus

Evan Spiegel's 2026 framing — "distribution has become the most important moat" — is the canonical recent treatment. Anchored in Spiegel ([Why distribution has become the most important moat, 2026-04-26](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/why-distribution-has-become-the-most-important-moat)) + Lenny "Finding your distribution advantage" (2021).

### Spiegel's argument (2026)
- **Software is no longer a moat.** Anyone can build software; AI has commoditized the "we built X" advantage.
- **Distribution is what's left.** Reach, relationships, channel access, brand presence, network effects.
- **Distribution compounds.** Each year of distribution advantage makes the next year easier.

### How distribution shows up as a moat
- **Network effects.** Each user makes the product more valuable to other users (Facebook, LinkedIn).
- **Brand recognition.** Customers think of you first / only (Apple, Stripe).
- **Channel exclusivity.** You're in the OEM stack; competitors can't easily replace you (Microsoft Office at scale, Apple App Store, Stripe inside major platforms).
- **Existing customer base.** Cross-sell to a million existing customers > selling to a million new ones.
- **Ecosystem position.** Cf. [ecosystem-channel](ecosystem-channel.md). Being the layer others build on.

### Mapping to Helmer's 7 Powers
Distribution appears across multiple powers in Helmer's framework:
- **Network effects** (one of the 7)
- **Branding** (another)
- **Cornered resource** (channel exclusivity sometimes)
- **Scale economies** (when distribution costs amortize)

### What this means for AI-era strategy
- **Foundation-model access is not a durable moat** for application-layer products. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google all expose APIs.
- **AI features as differentiation are commoditized within months.** Cf. Verna 4.0 — AI is table stakes, not differentiator.
- **Distribution-first strategy is increasingly the answer.** Lovable + others are betting on distribution alongside product.

### Lenny "Finding your distribution advantage" (2021)
- Identify what unique distribution access you have.
- Build the product to leverage that access.
- Don't compete on distribution dimensions where you have no advantage.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **The "AI is the moat" thesis has been revised down.** Specific AI features ship and get copied within months.
- **Distribution has been revised up.** Multiple corpus voices (Spiegel, Verna, Singhal) converge.
- **Ecosystem-as-channel** (cf. [ecosystem-channel](ecosystem-channel.md)) is now a recognized growth strategy.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO/founder**: ask honestly what distribution advantage you have. If "we'll do paid ads at scale" is the answer, you don't have a distribution advantage.
- **If you're an investor**: distribution-shaped pitches are increasingly credible. The "we have access to X channel" claim is worth probing.
- **If you're an exec at a non-AI-native company adding AI features**: don't expect AI features alone to differentiate. Distribution will determine which AI features win.
