# Copywriting and writing for product

## Current consensus

The corpus treats copywriting as a craft skill that PMs and founders should develop, not delegate. Anchored in Lenny corpus content + Wes Kao communication craft + Brandon Chu (Shopify) on writing.

### Why copywriting matters for builders
- **Every word users see** is conversion or friction.
- **The PM / founder voice** shapes brand. Outsourcing to agency produces generic.
- **Clarity in writing reflects clarity in thinking.** If you can't write it clearly, you don't understand it.

### The fundamentals
1. **Specificity beats abstraction.** "Save 4 hours per week" beats "save time."
2. **Customer language, not internal language.** Read your team's docs vs. customer-facing copy; they shouldn't sound the same.
3. **Active voice, short sentences.** Default; not always; usually.
4. **Edit ruthlessly.** First drafts are usually 30% too long.
5. **Read aloud.** If it doesn't read aloud naturally, it's awkward written too.

### Where copywriting lives
- **Onboarding flows.** Each screen's copy is conversion or churn.
- **Empty states.** Often the most important copy in the product.
- **Email + transactional.** Account confirmations, password resets — read by everyone.
- **Marketing site.** Hero, sub-hero, feature blocks.
- **Pricing page.** Often most edited surface; high stakes.

### Common failure modes
- **Generic SaaS-speak.** "Powerful, intuitive, all-in-one" — meaningless.
- **Internal jargon leaking.** Engineering / product vocabulary on customer-facing surfaces.
- **Long-form when short-form would do.** Most users skim; respect their time.
- **AI-generated copy shipped without revision.** Detectable; brand-eroding.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI tools generate first drafts in seconds.** Don't ship the first draft. Use as starting point; revise heavily.
- **AEO + answer-engine citation** (cf. [ai-search-aeo](ai-search-aeo.md)) rewards distinctive copy. Generic copy gets summarized away.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an engineer**: copy is part of the product. Don't push back on PM revisions to your error messages; they're product work.
- **If you're a designer**: copy and design are inseparable. Don't accept "we'll get a copywriter to fill it in later"; design with the actual words.
- **If you're a CEO**: invest in writing as a craft. Lenny corpus treats Brandon Chu's writing-as-PM-craft framing as canonical.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2020-2022**: Lenny corpus content on copywriting + community advice.
- **2024**: Wes Kao on writing-as-influence.
- **2025-2026**: AI-tooling for drafting; revision quality more important.
