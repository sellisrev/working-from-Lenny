# Product-led growth motion

## Current consensus

Anchored in Hila Qu ("The ultimate guide to adding a PLG motion", 2023) + Elena Verna 3.0 / 4.0 + Lenny "Five steps to starting your product-led growth motion" (2022).

### What PLG actually means
- **The product itself drives acquisition, activation, and revenue** — without sales-led handoffs.
- **Self-serve from signup to paying customer** for at least the entry tier.
- **Bottom-up adoption.** Individual users / small teams adopt; larger contracts emerge from organic growth.

### Hila Qu's guide
1. **Start with a working PLG funnel for the existing product.** Don't add PLG before the product can be self-served.
2. **Map the journey.** Acquisition → activation → engagement → conversion → expansion.
3. **Identify the friction.** PLG fails at the highest-friction step in the funnel.
4. **Instrument before optimizing.** Without analytics, PLG optimization is guessing.
5. **Iterate the entry tier.** Free / freemium tier design is the highest-leverage PLG decision. Cf. [freemium-vs-trial](freemium-vs-trial.md).

### Elena Verna's framing
- **PLG works when self-serve revenue is real.** If sales is the main revenue motion, calling yourself PLG is theater.
- **Hire growth before sales** for PLG companies.
- **Don't add PLG to a fundamentally sales-led product** unless you're willing to redesign the product.

### Common PLG failure modes
- **PLG without product-self-serve capability.** Forcing PLG onto a product that requires sales is doomed.
- **Freemium-as-default.** Some PLG products work freemium; others work trial. Cf. [freemium-vs-trial](freemium-vs-trial.md).
- **Premature sales overlay.** Adding sales before the PLG motion is solid kills the PLG motion.
- **Conversion fixation.** PLG is acquisition + activation + retention + monetization + expansion; over-indexing on one breaks the others.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-native consumer-prosumer PLG is dominant** in the 2024-2026 wave. Lovable, Cursor, Bolt, Replit all PLG-led for entry; sales overlay for enterprise.
- **The "hybrid bottom-up + sales overlay"** model (cf. [gtm-motions](gtm-motions.md)) has overtaken pure PLG as the dominant growth pattern at scale.
- **AI-feature monetization within PLG** (cf. [ai-pricing](ai-pricing.md)) creates new pricing experiments.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CEO contemplating PLG**: ask "is the product genuinely self-serve?" before committing. If not, redesign or stay sales-led.
- **If you're a sales leader at a PLG company**: your job is overlay on existing PLG signal, not pipeline-from-scratch generation.
- **If you're an investor evaluating PLG pitches**: ask for cohort-level conversion + retention data. PLG vanity metrics (signups, MAUs) without conversion / retention are weak signal.
