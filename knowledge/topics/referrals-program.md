# Building a referrals program

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "Building a referrals program" (2020) + Nilan Peiris (Wise) "How to drive word of mouth" (2023).

### When referrals programs work
- **The product already has organic word-of-mouth.** Referrals amplify; they don't create.
- **Referrer and referee both get value.** Two-sided incentives outperform one-sided.
- **Friction is low.** A 30-second invite flow beats a 5-minute one.
- **The reward is meaningful but doesn't dominate.** Token rewards generate gaming; large rewards distort acquisition.

### Nilan Peiris's word-of-mouth framing (Wise)
- **Word-of-mouth is built into the product**, not bolted on. Wise's product (international transfers) is inherently shareable because users save money and tell friends.
- **Measure recommendation rate, not just NPS.** "Did you tell someone about us?" is the durable metric.
- **Optimize the moment of recommendation.** What makes a user excited enough to share? Build for that moment.

### Lenny's referrals program canon (2020)
- **Two-sided incentives.** PayPal classic ($10 to give, $10 to receive).
- **Personalization beats generic.** "Sarah invited you" beats "Acme invites you."
- **Track referee quality, not just volume.** Spammy referrals dilute the network.
- **Have a moderation system.** Users will game the program.

### Common failure modes
- **Building referrals without organic recommendation.** Programs amplify; without underlying signal, they produce nothing.
- **One-sided rewards.** Only the referrer benefits — referees feel exploited.
- **Cash rewards that distort acquisition.** Suddenly you're acquiring users who only want the cash.
- **Forgetting the experience.** Referees who get a bad first impression churn fast.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-generated viral artifacts** function as implicit referrals. Cf. [virality](virality.md). Lovable apps with the "Built with Lovable" footer are referral mechanisms.
- **Programmatic referrals at scale** (Wise, Robinhood, Substack) continue to drive meaningful acquisition for products with strong PMF.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a marketing lead**: don't build a referrals program before you've measured organic word-of-mouth. The right sequence is signal → amplify.
- **If you're a CFO**: referrals are usually the highest-quality acquisition channel (lowest CAC, highest LTV) when working. Don't underinvest.
- **If you're an engineer**: friction in the referral flow is your enemy. Make it 30 seconds, not 5 minutes.
