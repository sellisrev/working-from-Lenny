# Bottom-up SaaS metrics and motion

## Current consensus

Anchored in Lenny "The most important bottom-up SaaS metrics to track" (2021) + Pete Kazanjy "The Transition: Layering sales onto a bottom-up self-serve product" (2022) + Hila Qu PLG canon.

### What "bottom-up SaaS" means
- **Individual users / small teams adopt the product** without procurement / IT involvement.
- **Adoption spreads laterally** within an organization through use, not top-down deployment.
- **Sales converts the lateral adoption into enterprise contracts** later. (The "PLG with sales overlay" pattern; cf. [gtm-motions](gtm-motions.md).)

### The most important bottom-up SaaS metrics (Lenny canonical)
1. **Free-to-paid conversion rate** — typical 2-5% for true freemium; 10-20% for trial-converting.
2. **Activation rate** — cf. [activation-metric](activation-metric.md). Critical for bottom-up because users self-serve.
3. **Account expansion** — net dollar retention > 110% at scale; ideally > 130%.
4. **Time to value** — days to first useful action; minutes to first wow moment for AI products.
5. **Viral coefficient** — within-account spread + cross-account spread.
6. **Land-and-expand revenue ratio** — revenue from existing accounts vs new acquisition.

### Pete Kazanjy "The Transition"
- **The transition from pure self-serve to layered sales is fragile.** Adding sales prematurely kills self-serve dynamics.
- **Sales should target accounts with high product engagement.** "Hand-raising" via product signal beats outbound.
- **Self-serve never goes away.** Even at $100M+ ARR, self-serve continues to be the primary acquisition motion.

### Common failure modes
- **Expecting bottom-up to scale to enterprise without sales overlay.** Most products top out at SMB without sales.
- **Layering sales too early.** Kills self-serve flywheel.
- **Pricing the product for sales but distributing it bottom-up.** Friction at the wrong layer.
- **Expansion without ARR floors.** Companies expand into accounts then can't justify enterprise pricing.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-native bottom-up** is the dominant 2024-2026 wave. Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, ChatGPT Pro all bottom-up.
- **Outcome-based AI pricing** complicates traditional bottom-up SaaS metrics.
- **The hybrid (bottom-up + sales)** model has overtaken pure bottom-up at scale.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a CFO at a bottom-up SaaS**: NDR is your single most important durable metric.
- **If you're a sales lead at a bottom-up SaaS**: your job is product-signal-driven outbound (PLS — product-led sales), not cold-outbound from scratch.
- **If you're a CEO contemplating "should we go bottom-up?"**: bottom-up requires the product to genuinely self-serve. Most products labeled "bottom-up" actually require sales help.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021-2022**: Lenny "Most important bottom-up SaaS metrics" + Pete Kazanjy "The Transition" — canonical.
- **2023-2024**: Hila Qu PLG canon expansion.
- **2024-2026**: AI-native bottom-up wave; hybrid model dominance.
