# Demand-driving-supply growth loop

## Current consensus

Lenny "Demand driving supply: The little-understood growth loop behind a surprising number of iconic billion-dollar companies" (2022) is the canonical corpus reference.

### The loop
- **Demand attracts supply.** When users keep showing up looking for X, suppliers come to meet them.
- **Booking.com** is the canonical example: travelers searching → hotels listing → more inventory → more travelers.
- **Airbnb** at certain phases: travelers searching destinations → hosts listing → more travelers.
- **Amazon** in its third-party era: shoppers searching → sellers joining → catalog grows → more shoppers.

### Why it's "little understood"
Most growth-loop discussions focus on supply-driving-demand patterns (Uber growth playbook). Demand-driving-supply is rarer but powerful when it applies.

### When the loop works
- **There's frustrated unmet demand.** Clear signal that users want something not currently available.
- **Suppliers can be acquired through demand-side traffic.** Search rankings, marketplace placement, etc., make demand visible to suppliers.
- **Network effects compound the loop.** Each new supplier increases the value of demand visits.

### When the loop fails
- **Supply is hard to acquire regardless of demand.** Some markets need active supply-side recruiting.
- **Demand-side users churn before supply scales.** Slow loop dies before it works.
- **Marketplace dynamics fight against the loop.** If listing on your platform is unattractive, demand alone won't pull supply.

### What's changed in 2025-2026
- **AI-mediated marketplaces** can compress the loop. AI-generated listings, AI customer service, AI matching — each step makes the loop faster.
- **The Mercor pattern** (cf. [evals-for-ai-products](evals-for-ai-products.md)): demand from AI labs for expert evals attracts experts; specialized demand-driving-supply within an emerging market.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're a marketplace founder**: ask whether your loop is supply-driving-demand or demand-driving-supply. Different playbooks apply.
- **If you're an investor**: Booking-style demand-driving-supply marketplaces are rarer but generate disproportionate returns when they work.
- **If you're an operator at a marketplace**: SEO + content (cf. [seo-strategy](seo-strategy.md)) is often the demand-side amplifier. AEO is now the 2026 equivalent.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2022-04**: Lenny "Demand driving supply" — canonical corpus piece.
- **2022-2024**: marketplace-specific applications (Booking.com case studies, Airbnb).
- **2025-2026**: AI-mediated marketplace patterns; Mercor as new exemplar.
