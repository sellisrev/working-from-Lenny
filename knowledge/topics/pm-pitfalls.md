# Common pitfalls of new PMs

## Current consensus

The corpus codified five canonical pitfalls (Lenny, 2021), supplemented by Camille Fournier's engineer-side perspective (2024) and Cat Wu's 2026 framing.

### The five canonical pitfalls (Lenny, 2021)
1. **The Coordinator** — treats themselves as a project manager; specs are about who-does-what-by-when, not why.
2. **The Dictator** — fully embraces "mini-CEO"; micromanages; defensive when challenged.
3. **The Dreamer** — vision without execution; perpetual 6+ month roadmaps with little shipped.
4. **The Feature Factory** — ships everything sales / leadership asks for; reactive not strategic.
5. **The Busted Umbrella** — fails to shield the team from chaos; perpetual P0 thrash.

### The five most annoying habits (Lenny, 2021)
6. **Obsessing over frameworks** — applies Twitter-celebrity framework to a team that doesn't fit.
7. **Prioritizing impulsively** — anecdotal data drives roadmap.
8. **Expecting everything to be quick** — doesn't understand engineering effort.
9. **Avoiding decisions** — punts hard calls back to the team.
10. **Being too prescriptive** — see Dictator.

### The four engineer-side complaints (Fournier, 2024)
11. **Hoarding credit.**
12. **Not understanding details and acting like they don't matter.**
13. **Not involving engineers in ideation.**
14. **Triggering major rewrites without understanding the cost.**

### The 2026 addition
15. **Not engaging with AI tooling.** A 2026 PM who refuses to use Cursor / Claude Code is increasingly unable to do their own job. Cf. [ai-pm-skills](ai-pm-skills.md).

### How to avoid these pitfalls
- **Adopt the affirmative habits**: 14 habits of highly effective PMs (Lenny). Customer obsession, crisp judgment, bias to action, etc. Cf. [top-1-percent-pm](top-1-percent-pm.md).
- **Get coaching.** Most pitfalls are coachable in 90 days with feedback.
- **Read your own performance reviews carefully.** Patterns appear if you let them.
- **Ask peers what's annoying.** They know; they're often relieved you asked.

### When pitfalls are hire-mistakes vs coaching opportunities
- **Coachable**: Coordinator pattern (mentorship reframes), Dreamer (delivery training), framework-obsession (1:1 conversation usually fixes), AI-disengagement (workshop fixes).
- **Often hire-mistakes**: deep Dictator pattern (rarely changes), persistent integrity issues, refusal to learn.

## What this means for non-PM consumers

- **If you're an engineer or designer working with a PM exhibiting these patterns**: direct feedback is the first move. Cf. [spotting-bad-pm-behaviors](spotting-bad-pm-behaviors.md).
- **If you're a manager of PMs**: most of these are coachable; identify the pattern, name it, set 90-day improvement goals.
- **If you're a CEO whose product team is full of these patterns**: it's a hiring / leadership problem, not individual-PM problems.

## How thinking has evolved

- **2021**: Lenny "Most common pitfalls of new product managers" + "Five habits of highly annoying" — foundational taxonomy.
- **2024-09**: Fournier engineer-side complaints.
- **2025-2026**: AI-disengagement added as 15th pitfall.
