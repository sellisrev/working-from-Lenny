# Obsolete advice: SEO strategy in the post-AI era

## "SEO is the lowest-cost growth channel; just write content and rank for keywords"

- **Claim**: Standard 2020-2023 startup SEO advice. "Free traffic" was the framing; programmatic content + keyword research was the playbook.
- **Originally said by**: Brian Ta (2020-07-14, [Winning at SEO](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/winning-at-seo)); reinforced across many programmatic-SEO case studies in the corpus.
- **Why obsolete**: Eli Schwartz (2024-09): Google AI Overviews now consume the click for many informational queries, so the long-tail-content-factory model produces less traffic per dollar. Google's Helpful Content updates have specifically penalized commodity content. The "free" framing also ignored that quality SEO content has always cost real labor; in the AI era the labor cost rises while the traffic return falls.
- **Where it may still apply**:
  - For high-intent transactional queries (e.g., "buy [product] online", "[brand] login") where the user wants to navigate, not get an answer.
  - For categories where the established SEO winner has accumulated authority (Notion, Webflow, Airtable, Stripe still win their categories).
  - In non-English markets where AI search penetration is lower.
- **Replaced by**: brand + authority + original research + AEO; see [Current consensus](../topics/seo-strategy.md#current-consensus).
- **Confidence in retirement**: high — multi-source recency, Google's own framing has shifted.

## "Programmatic SEO at scale: build a page for every long-tail variant"

- **Claim**: 2020-2023 playbook exemplified by Zapier (140k pages), MasterClass, Canva, Notion-template-style strategies.
- **Originally said by**: Brian Ta and others; widely held in corpus.
- **Why obsolete**: The model still works for the brands that built it pre-2024 (their authority compounds). For new entrants attempting the same model, the marginal returns have collapsed. AI Overviews consume the click; the long-tail page generates impressions but not clicks.
- **Where it may still apply**:
  - For products with genuinely unique data per page (Zillow per-property pages, real-estate aggregators, marketplaces with unique inventory).
  - For categories where the user must navigate to a specific page to take action (booking, search filter, etc.).
- **Replaced by**: more selective programmatic SEO; bigger investments in unique data / proprietary signals per page; see [Current consensus](../topics/seo-strategy.md#current-consensus).
- **Confidence in retirement**: medium-high.

## "Backlinks are the most important ranking signal"

- **Claim**: Pre-2023 SEO orthodoxy.
- **Originally said by**: industry-wide.
- **Why obsolete**: Google has discounted link-driven ranking for several years; AI answer engines weight authority and citation patterns differently. Buying or aggressive link-building is high-detection-risk and low-benefit.
- **Where it may still apply**:
  - Backlinks from genuinely authoritative domains (industry publications, .gov, .edu) still matter.
  - For topical authority signaling, citations FROM authoritative content count more than links TO your domain.
- **Replaced by**: brand mentions, citations in authoritative content, original research that gets cited; see [Current consensus](../topics/seo-strategy.md#current-consensus).
- **Confidence in retirement**: medium.

## "Write 3000-word long-form content to rank for informational queries"

- **Claim**: 2020-2023 content marketing canon: longer is better, comprehensive coverage wins ranking.
- **Originally said by**: industry-wide.
- **Why obsolete**: AI Overviews extract the answer from your long-form content; the user satisfies their query without clicking. CTR on first-page results for informational queries has dropped substantially (industry reports cite 30-60% declines in some categories).
- **Where it may still apply**:
  - For genuinely complex topics where users want to read and learn (technical tutorials, deep analyses) — the click still happens because the user wants depth.
  - For brand-building content where the goal isn't traffic but authority signaling (which you'll then leverage in AEO).
- **Replaced by**: original research, expert interviews, opinion content with a strong author brand; see [Current consensus](../topics/seo-strategy.md#current-consensus).
- **Confidence in retirement**: medium.
